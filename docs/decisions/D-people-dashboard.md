**D-people-dashboard. The People page is a relationship dashboard; the Address Book is the roster.**
The People section page stops listing Person rows (the grid duplicated the Address Book, and a synced address book drowned it in acquaintances) — a one-line Address Book link replaces it, and the three surfaces divide cleanly: Address Book = everyone + import/sync + CRUD, person page = the per-person CRM (profile, notes, log contact, Reconnect), section page = the social dashboard, shipped as the People lead panel under the D37 convention (whose base gained `hidden_browse` — the browse-zone counterpart of `hidden_tool_slugs` — so a panel can replace generic cards it renders with better context). The dashboard's subject is the **circle, not the rolodex**: `people.keep_in_touch` (explicit, manual, set from the person page or the section's "Who do you want to stay close to?" capture card — the Q8 no-inference rule extended, since an import must not turn 300 contacts into 300 obligations) selects who appears; `last_contacted_on` stays the only recency signal, and flagged-but-never-logged people show on the page but never trigger the watcher (no signal, no nudge). The stay-in-touch watcher is circle-scoped, with the migration backfilling the flag wherever `last_contacted_on` is set (logging contact was the opt-in until now) so nobody's nudges change on upgrade day; the circle itself is personal (own people, not `visible_to` — the flag is the owner's intent). The panel is capture / Coming up (birthdays off `people.birthday` + People-tagged events) / Your circle (stalest first, one-tap Log contact + Reconnect) / groups (People's sub-lenses — D25 generalized into `Sections::Lens`). Full design in `docs/PEOPLE.md`. — *Built (the split, birthday radar, the four-tool slate + caregiver/bereavement Situations, groups); Q17 links, the afterglow watcher, the cadence upgrade, and community tooling remain open behind explicit wait conditions.*
