**D-food-section. Food is the tenth top-level section; the recipe box stays in the Vault.**
Amends D-recipe-primitive's "not an eighth section", by request. What changed isn't the recipe — food grew three things that aren't recipes (shared shopping lists, the fridge, the week's meals) and they need a *place*; since D-travel-facet and D-pets-section, a section is how this app makes one without a silo: one seeded row buys the sidebar entry, the Settings toggle, the agent dial, the tool registry, the section grant and the browse zone. What D-recipe-primitive protected still holds. Recipes keep multi-tagging (the low-sodium soup is still Health); `Sections::Panels::Food` reads `Recipe.visible_to(viewer)` directly the way the Pets panel reads `Pet`, so the hub never depends on a recipe carrying the Food tag — that tag is for the things that are *about* food without being food objects (the delivery receipt, the dinner party, the allergist's letter). The library stays at `/recipes` in the Vault, as `/photos` did when Travel arrived: the Vault is where you browse the collection, the section is where the life-area happens. No subsections at launch. Mechanics copy D-pets-section: a seed row at position 10 plus an `AddFoodSection` migration for running boxes, with the same slug-collision handling. — *Designed; F1 of `docs/FOOD_PLAN.md`.*
