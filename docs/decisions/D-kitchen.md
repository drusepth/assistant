**D-kitchen. The kitchen is one shared container; its lists, fridge and meal plan are its rows, and a grant on it lets the grantee cook.**
Amends D-pantry's one-pantry-per-user. The container half was right; the per-user half breaks the moment there's a shopping list, because a household has one fridge and every grant in the app is a read grant, so the list two spouses most need to share would still be read-only to one of them. So the unit is a `Kitchen` (`Visible`, in `AccessGrant::ITEM_TYPES`, owner-named — "Home", "The cabin", "My shelf") whose `pantry_items`, `shopping_lists` and `meals` are rows with no owner of their own, visible exactly when the kitchen is. **One grant shares all of it, and it is the first grant that writes**: "you can cook here" — add to and check off the lists, update the fridge, fill in the plan — while renaming, deleting and re-sharing stay with the owner. The share card says so in those words (D9), and the permission lives in the kitchen's controllers rather than as a permission level on every `AccessGrant`, because no read-only kitchen share is worth offering and a grant-level permission is a much bigger decision. This sidesteps Q-recipe-collab and Q15 rather than answering them: those are hard because a contributed *primitive* has an owner, and a list line doesn't (`added_by`/`checked_by` are for display, never permission). The first Food visit creates "Home" and asks, once and visibly, whether to share it with the household — nothing shared until the click. **Meals are kitchen rows, not Events**, reversing `RECIPES_PLAN.md` §5's `events.recipe_id`: an Event per dinner is a separately-owned, separately-granted private item your spouse can't move, which is the per-item-grant absurdity D-pantry rejected relocated to the calendar. The Calendar draws the plan as a band, as it does trips (D-trip-spine); a meal that really is an event (the dinner party) links to one. Every kitchen write broadcasts over Turbo Streams, because two people are in it at once. — *Designed; F1–F5 of `docs/FOOD_PLAN.md`.*
