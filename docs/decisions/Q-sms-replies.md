**Q-sms-replies.** The phone channels are one-way (D-sms-channels): the assistant can alert, but it cannot ask "should I book the 3pm?" and get an answer back. Two designs, and the choice matters more than it looks. **A signed link** — the text carries the usual counts-and-titles pointer plus a one-tap URL with a single-use `signed_id` landing on a decision page — needs no inbound webhook, so it works behind NAT on every provider including the modem, and the decision is authenticated by a real session rather than by a phone number. **Reply-to-answer** reads better on a phone but needs a publicly reachable inbound endpoint (a tunnel, or the LAN-side gateway that can post inwards), and sender ID is trivially spoofable, so an inbound message could never be authorization on its own: it would have to match a pending question's nonce, be rate-limited, and be incapable of creating anything — D20's "the agent proposes, the user disposes" held at the SMS boundary. Lean signed-link first. — *Not started.*
