**D-document-intake. A document proposes; the review page disposes. The file is always kept; nothing else lands without the click.**
A photo of the vet's discharge sheet uploads, files to Rex in one click, and then sits there: `Documents::Analyze` reads PDFs and text only, and even for a PDF it produces a summary and a section suggestion and nothing the visit implies — the vaccination doesn't reach the care radar, the booster's due date doesn't reach the calendar, the prescription diet doesn't reach the food shelf, the vet doesn't reach the Address Book, the weight doesn't reach the pet. Retyping what the app is already holding is the failure, and it is general (a lease, a lab report, an insurance card, a school letter), not a pet one. So: **the document is the run.** Analysis reads the file — text, text-bearing PDF, or an image and scanned PDF through the vision rung `Photos::Caption` already uses, with an honest "this model can't read images" when no vision model is declared rather than a guess — and, against the landing registry (D-landing-registry), writes what it found into `documents.landings`: one validated entry per proposed row, with a code-computed fingerprint and status `proposed`. `/documents/:id/review` shows them as a checklist, every entry checked, each with its kind's preview and the filing it would get, under the `_prepared.html.erb` line that nothing on the page exists yet; Save calls the checked kinds' `land!` in one transaction and records provenance in `document_items` (the `tool_run_items` shape), so a row can say "from the vet report of 12 Aug — your word beats the reader's". Unchecked entries are marked `skipped`, not deleted: re-analysis never re-proposes a fingerprint already saved or skipped, and the page keeps a way back to them. Three rules the copy makes true: **nothing lands without the click** beyond the D16 filing that already exists (section suggestions on an untagged document, the model's title on a filename-titled one — both overwritable, neither a row); **the file is always kept**, so skipping everything is a complete outcome and a receipt you only wanted in the vault is not a failed intake; and **a landing inherits the document's filing** — upload from Rex's page and everything it produces is tagged Rex, plus what the kind adds — which is inheritance, not classification. Facts stay distinct from landings: `extracted_facts["pay_stub"]` is what *this document says*, consumed against the document, and D-career-signal's automatic comp-change event is unchanged. A stated due date lands as a future all-day event with `care_kind: none`, so the calendar gives lead time without the care radar reading a due date as a booking (Q-intake-stated-due is whether the radar should learn to hold it).
