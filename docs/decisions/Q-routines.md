**Q-routines.** Should a chore be able to *appear on the board* on its cadence — "take the trash out" reopened and pulled into Today each Thursday, checked off, gone until next week? D-standing-reminders deliberately put recurrence on the Reminder and not on Task, because a respawning task fights the done/not-done invariant and the staleness markers (`docs/REMINDERS_PLAN.md` §2). If a household asks for the chore on the board rather than just the ping, the standing reminder is the cadence it would ride: one durable Task row, one rule, the firing also moving the task into Today. Decide it on a real ask, not in advance.
