**D-review-scheduler. Review items are first-class rows scheduled by FSRS; the schedule is arithmetic, the model only writes and grades cards.**
Retrieval practice plus spacing is the highest-leverage mechanism in the education plan, so it must work on a box with no model configured — the D20 split: `review_items` (prompt/answer rows attached to topics, minted by quizzes, tutor session mining, or by hand) carry FSRS memory state — stability, difficulty, due date — and the scheduler is pure deterministic arithmetic in `app/services/reviews/`. FSRS is the open modern standard (Anki's default since 23.10), chosen over SM-2 for materially better interval prediction with published parameters. Self-grading (Again/Hard/Good/Easy) needs no model at all; a configured model additionally *writes* better cards from what a session covered and *grades* free-response answers — phrasing and judgement, never the decision. The daily queue interleaves across all subjects by construction (interleaving beats blocked practice), targets ~10 phone-sized minutes, and the same due-count query feeds the hub's lead card and the `reviews_due` watcher. Lapses drive the `rusty` demotion in D-mastery-evidence. Full design in `EDUCATION_PLAN.md`. — *Built (W3): the scheduler and its property-test battery, the /reviews surface, the rusty sweep, the reviews_due watcher. Self-grading only in v1; model grading of typed answers stays a W6 nicety.*
