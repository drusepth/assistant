**D-diary-vault. DiaryEntry is a vault container; everything sealed except the date.**
The diary joins the sidebar's Vault group as a **Diary** view over a new `DiaryEntry` model on the **Album/Credential precedent**: `belongs_to :user`, `Visible`, **not** `Sectionable` — no categorizer, no tool context, no dashboard mention, and no section grant can structurally ever match. It goes one tier further than D-passwords-vault: **no AR-metadata tier at all** — title, body, and any user-typed mood word live inside the envelope-encrypted payload; the plaintext `entry_date` is the one deliberate metadata leak (the `totp_present` pattern), so a locked index shows dates, calendar, and streak and nothing else. A per-user **diary key** (wrapped under the vault key) prices owner reads at a GCM unwrap instead of an RSA unwrap, so a thousand-entry journal lists and searches in one request on Pi-class hardware; per-entry item keys stay, wrapped under the diary key for the owner (with an RSA capture-while-locked scheme) and RSA-OAEP per grantee for W4's per-entry, read-only, rotate-on-revoke sharing. Privacy posture: private absolutely by default — sharing is per-entry only; **emergency access excludes the diary by default** behind a per-user dial whose copy says plainly that the exclusion is app policy, not cryptography; AI reads are structurally impossible in v1 (chat rides background jobs, key material never rides the queue) and any future dial is per-user, off by default, in-request only (Q-diary-ai). The recovery kit is a **gate before the first entry**, not a nag — a diary lost to a password reset is years of writing, not re-keyable accounts. Full design in `DIARY_PLAN.md`. — *Built (waves 0–4: the sealed container and diary-key tier, the habit surfaces, import/export, per-entry sharing and the emergency dial). The build made the diary key an argument rather than model state after a parked key silently decrypted past a vault reset; whole-diary loss is detected by the crypto with a stamp only for the banner; and the dials sit behind the unlock, because a settings row created while locked is indistinguishable from one a password reset broke. W5 (AI, attachments) stays gated on Q-diary-ai and Q-diary-attachments.*
