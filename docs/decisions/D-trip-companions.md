**D-trip-companions. Trips share by membership, not AccessGrant — and nothing coordinate-derived ever crosses users.**
A household trip is the first thing in the app that two people genuinely *do together*, rather than one person granting the other a read-only window — so it gets a membership row (`TripMembership`, household-checked, owner implicit) instead of joining `AccessGrant::ITEM_TYPES`. Companions see the trip, edit the itinerary, comment (`TripComment`, the app's first commentable surface — author-or-owner delete), and post photos; the owner alone renames, re-dates, deletes, and manages the roster (companions can always remove themselves). What membership deliberately does **not** share is anything derived from photo coordinates: the trip map and the derived camera roll stay owner-only, which is Q14 held for the third time rather than quietly widened (the map and D-travel-facet held it first). Photos cross users only by the explicit act of **posting** (`TripPhoto`): each traveller's picker offers their *own* photos filtered by the trip's dates — dates, never the box, so nothing location-derived leaks even structurally — and a posted photo becomes visible to co-travellers through a new `Photo.extra_visibility_clauses` OR-branch guarded like the album branch (poster must own the photo), arriving EXIF-stripped like every shared variant. The per-album "share locations" toggle stays Q14's open half; if it ever lands, a trip choosing to share its map is the feature that would consume it.
