**D-pet-primitive. A pet is a member of the household, and each pet owns a section lens.**
Pets were the biggest hole in the life-tracking surface — vet care, food, cost, papers and photographs, none of it addressable — and the whole design turns on refusing the obvious answer. **A pet is not a section.** Sections are the *what* axis; a pet is a *whom*. File the vet appointment under a top-level "Pets" and it stops being Health (off the care radar, out of the insurance card's reach); file the kibble there and it leaves the budget. Every surface that already works would have to be rebuilt inside the silo, which is the definition of the standalone feature this was asked not to be. As a lens it is `Health + Rex`, and both pages are right. It is equally not a `Person` with a species column: Person is a *contact* (email, phone, `keep_in_touch`, the Address Book, the stay-in-touch watcher), and a pet filed there gets a nudge to reconnect with the cat. So: a `Pet` record on the D5 contract (`belongs_to :user`, `Visible`, `Sectionable`, `Avatarable`) carrying species, breed, dates, markings, microchip and vet — **plus a `custom: true` lens under People**, created and renamed with it through the existing `Sections::Lens`. The lens is the load-bearing half, because it makes "about Rex" a *tag*: every form's section picker can already file to her, `in_section` is "everything about Rex" across every primitive including Photos (so "pictures of my dog" needs no `photo_pets` table), and per-pet sharing is an ordinary section grant — which makes the household pet the most natural grant in the app. Sharing is offered as one checkbox ("Everyone in the household looks after Rex") that writes visible, revocable grant rows; nothing is implied by the household relationship itself (D9). Because a lens answers "which pet", pets do **not** force Q17 — its real consumer is still attendees, many-per-item with per-person history. Three consequences: `Sections::Panel.panel_class` gains a model-backed rung so a pet's lens resolves to `Panels::PetLens` instead of the group-members page every custom People child currently gets; renaming a pet renames the lens but never its slug (slugs are a global URL namespace, and `available_slug` already numbers the second Luna); and `died_on` stops every radar, watcher and reorder while keeping the record, the photos and the history, moving the card to a quiet Remembered strip — the app never asks a bereaved owner to confirm a deletion, and the one thing it offers is the memorial album the tags already assembled. Zero pets is zero pixels: no section toggle, because every pet surface is gated on there being a pet. — *Built 2026-08 (W1, and the lens carries W4's photos and papers for free). Two things the build changed: a pet's lens is **created**, never found — `Sections::Lens.create!` joins `find_or_create!`, because the second dog called Rex must not take the first one's section (usually one remembered and one new), and takes `rex-2`; and `Section#removable?` had to exist, because the generic "Remove this group" button sat on every custom child of People and would have orphaned the pet who owned it. See `docs/PETS_PLAN.md`.*
