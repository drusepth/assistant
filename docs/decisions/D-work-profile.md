**D-work-profile. Job title and employer are user-profile fields, stated once and read by every Career surface.**
`users.job_title` + `users.employer`, free text, edited on your own Settings page ("Your work"). The D-household-profile bar applies — a field earns its place with **named consumers**, and these have many: `Tool::Base#work_line` injects the stated work into every Career tool prompt (raise case, interview prep, negotiation prep, mock interview, tailor-application, decode-offer, pay-stub check, wage claim, find-my-next-move); the two plan tools fold it into their goal descriptions; the job scout ranks postings against it; and the raise case both pre-fills its job-title field from it (the new `prefill:` input option — form seeding on `new` only, so a deliberately cleared field stays cleared on submit) and falls back to it for the BLS wage-table match. Per-user, not per-household — spouses have different jobs — and **private to the user like any other item**: it shows on your own Settings and Career panel, never on the household page. The Q8 discipline is untouched: this is the user *stating* a fact, not the app inferring one, so prompts may treat it as ground truth (`work_line` says so) while blank still means tools ask or leave placeholders. It rides the export manifest with name and email. The work-history Note ("Tell me about your work", CAREER.md §1) keeps the narrative — skills, history, comp story; these two fields are just the header line, cheap enough to ask for in one card and load-bearing enough to thread everywhere. — *Built.*
