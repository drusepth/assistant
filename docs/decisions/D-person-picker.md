**D-person-picker. Adding a person is a type-to-find combobox, never a select over the address book**

Every place that puts a person somewhere — the circle capture, each group card, a group's page, a photo's tags — used a `<select>` of everyone the viewer could see, plus a separate "…or someone new" text field. Fine at two contacts; unusable the day a CardDAV or phone import drops four hundred in, and the People page rendered the whole roster into its HTML twice. Now it is one field, `Ui::PersonPickerComponent`: type a few letters, the server answers with up to eight option rows (`People::MatchesController`, rendered through `Ui::AvatarComponent` so faces look the same as everywhere else), names that *start* with the query first, circle before rolodex, and people already in the circle/group/photo left out. The last row offers "Add “X”" as a new contact unless someone already has that exact name. Picking submits; Enter with nothing highlighted submits the typed name. `People::Picker` owns the pool, the search and `resolve` for all four places; `resolve` reuses an exact (case-insensitive) name before creating, so Enter-without-picking — and the no-JS path, where the field is a plain name input — can't mint a duplicate Rosa. Nothing is auto-highlighted except an exact match, so Enter on "Ann" never quietly adds "Anna Lee". This is search over the user's own rows, not classification, so D-no-keyword-logic doesn't reach it. Alongside it, the People page lost its duplicate capture: the "Who do you want to stay close to?" card leads only while the circle is empty, and once it isn't, the circle card's "Add someone" is the one way in. Photo tagging can now create the contact too.

**Second pass — filling a circle in one sitting.** The picker's own forms send `inline=1` and are answered with Turbo Streams (`PersonPicked` concern): the card's summary (`#<card>-summary`), Reach out, or a group's member rows are redrawn in place, the notice lands in `#flash` with an **Undo** (a DELETE — `FlashComponent` undos now take a method) and, for a contact just made from a bare name, an "Add a birthday or phone" link; the field clears and keeps the cursor. Anything else posting to the same endpoints (the person page, no JS) keeps its redirect and the same Undo. Focusing the empty field offers up to six **suggestions** from plain facts — most-tagged in your photos, last talked to, birthday within 45 days, added by hand this week (skipped when more than 20 were added, i.e. an import) — each showing its reason. Results bold the matched text, and a name that appears twice always carries a hint (relationship, else email, phone, or the address's last line). On a phone the picker becomes a full-screen sheet while focused (`.picker-sheet`), since a dropdown opens under the keyboard. Around it: People and group pages drop the contradictory "Nothing in … yet" card (`show_empty_browse? = false`), a new `Panel#lead_tool_slugs` hook keeps three tools in view and folds the rest under "More tools", the card's "Add someone" became a "+" beside the faces, "Start a group" became a quiet "+ New group" tile that lands you on the new group's page with the cursor in "Who's in …?", and "Log contact" is now "Talked today" everywhere.

**Third pass — every contact field, not just the People page.** The tool forms' `kind: :person` inputs (Reconnect, Gift ideas, What do I say) and the pet form's Vet field were still a `<select>` / `<datalist>` of the whole address book. Both are now the picker in its form-field mode (`submit_on_pick: false`, `place: :any` → `People::Picker.for_anyone`): picking fills the field and the form moves on. A tool input posts the pick as `inputs[<name>]` and the typed text as `person_names[<name>]`; `ToolRunsController` resolves a typed name the same way every picker does (exact name reused, else a new contact of the viewer's own), so "Gift ideas for Cousin Ada" works before Ada is in the Address Book. The Vet field posts only the name — `Pets::VetContact` already matches or creates the contact on save. Left as selects on purpose: the vaccination form's vet list (only contacts marked Vet — a handful) and household-member pickers (bounded by the household).
