**D-goal-pause. Goals can be paused without shame, and quiet goals say so; the agent check-in waits for Milestone 3.**
The number-one killer of long-term retention in goal products is the guilt museum: goals stall, the list becomes a wall of 0% reproaches, opening the app starts to feel bad, and the user churns. Two defenses land now. **Paused** joins the status enum (active / done / paused): a paused goal leaves the board's strip entirely and parks in a Paused shelf on the Goals tab, one click each way, no confirmation ceremony — life has seasons, and "learn guitar" shelved for December is a plan, not a failure. **Staleness is surfaced, gently**: an active goal with no step completed (and no plan activity) in six weeks (`Goal::STALE_AFTER`) carries a quiet "no movement in 6 weeks" marker on its card and sorts into the needs-attention band of the strip — a nudge toward re-planning, pausing, or finishing, phrased as information rather than a red badge. Both are computed from timestamps already written (`completed_at`, `broken_down_at`, `created_at`); no new counters, no model call. The third defense — the assistant *asking* ("this hasn't moved in six weeks; still matter? want a fresh plan, or shelf it?") — is designed in `docs/TASKS_PAGE_PLAN.md` but deliberately deferred to the Milestone 3 agent work (D20), where check-ins get their aggressiveness dial; a nudge that can't be dialed is a nag.
