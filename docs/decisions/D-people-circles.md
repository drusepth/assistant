**D-people-circles. Every collection of people is a card; the circle is one of them.**
The People page said "here are the people in your life" three times in three visual languages: groups as `SectionChipComponent` chips (a filter token, not a place — no faces, no count, no next thing, and printing `qualified_name` so every one read "People · Church"), the circle as a full-width card of person rows (special-cased because it was built first, not because it differs in kind), and the Address Book as a text link. They collapse into **one grid of one card**: an avatar stack of up to five overlapping member faces plus a `+N` bubble, the plain name, one line of signal (next group event, else a birthday inside it, else what's in flight, else an honest invitation), and two actions that end in contact — `Add someone` and `Plan something` (prefilling GetTogether's already-free-text `who`). The circle is simply the first card, wearing an `Only you` chip. The circle does **not** become a `Section` to make that uniform: sections are household-shared taxonomy while `keep_in_touch` is per-user intent (D-people-dashboard), so sharing it would be exactly the hidden default D9 forbids — they unify at the presentation layer via a `People::Circles::Card` struct and nothing deeper. The rows the circle earned survive as a trimmed **Been a while** list (the stalest few, one-tap Log contact + Reconnect — the pull-side twin of the stay-in-touch watcher); the full circle is one tap away as an Address Book filter rather than a new page. The redesign's functional half is the verb that was missing entirely: group membership is a `section_tagging` whose only writer was the section picker in the person form's Advanced drawer, so groups were born empty and stayed that way — `People::Membership` plus `Add someone` on every card and a Groups line on the person page makes a group something you can actually put someone in. New `Ui::AvatarStackComponent` renders *through* `Ui::AvatarComponent` (one face, one place — D-user-avatars) and is claimed in advance by photo albums, household member lists, and Q17 attendees. Full design in `docs/PEOPLE_CIRCLES.md`. — *Planned; wave 1 (the grid) and wave 2 (membership) not yet built.*
