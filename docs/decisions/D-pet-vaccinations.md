**D-pet-vaccinations. A pet's vaccines are dated dose records with their own good-until dates, not events.**
The Routine care tile ran one 12-month clock for "Vaccinations" and found them by matching words in event titles, so it was wrong for most pets (rabies and DHPP are often three years, kennel cough six to twelve months) and could not answer the questions people actually get asked: which vaccines, until when, and where is the certificate. The visit stays an Event; what was *given* at it becomes a `PetVaccination` row per dose — or per titer or vet exemption, which count as coverage and are labelled as what they are — carrying the vaccine (a per-species catalog key, or free text for species the catalog doesn't know), given and good-until dates, product, lot, tag number, site, series dose, the vet, the certificate, and any reaction, which is surfaced as a warning and never collapsed. "Current" is a query over the latest dose per vaccine, never a column. `due_on` comes from the certificate or from a duration the owner picks; the app never assumes one, because a guessed duration either nags years early or lets a pet board unvaccinated. The catalog (`Pets::Vaccines`) states the professional bodies' core/lifestyle labels as reference and encodes no local law and no recommendation. Reminders come from a watcher over good-until dates that collapses one visit's worth of vaccines into one nudge and retires itself when a newer dose is logged or an appointment is booked. The shareable record joins `ShareLink::SHAREABLE_TYPES` on the recipe terms — owner-made, expiring, revocable, and showing only the vaccine sheet's fields. Plan: `docs/PET_VACCINES_PLAN.md`. — *Built 2026-09 (W1–W4). What the build changed is in the plan's §12: a booked visit is the `care_kind` the event already had rather than a new column, the share subject is the Pet itself, and the due-soon lead is 30 days or a third of the dose's interval, whichever is shorter.*
