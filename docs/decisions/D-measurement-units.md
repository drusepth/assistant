**D-measurement-units. Weights and distances are shown and typed in the user's chosen units (imperial by default); storage stays metric**
One per-user setting in Settings — "Pounds and miles" or "Kilograms and kilometres" (`users.measurement_system`, default imperial, since the people running this today are American) — governs every weight and distance the app shows or asks for: a pet's weight on its page and form, a place's radius, how far away an event is, the vet-sheet landing's old → new preview, and the pet context a tool sends the model. Storage does not move: `pets.weight_kg` and `places.radius_km` stay metric, because the model reads them (the landing asks for kilograms), the radar's distance maths and the listing providers speak kilometres, and a column whose unit depends on who last saved it is a bug waiting to happen. `Units` is the one place a stored number meets the reader's system, both ways; forms post `weight` / `radius` in the reader's unit and the controller converts, while `weight_kg` / `radius_km` are still accepted directly. Precisions are chosen so a round trip never drifts (0.01 kg under a 0.1 lb display, a whole km under a whole mile). Per user rather than per household, because it is a reading preference, not shared data. Pet-food amounts are untouched — each row already carries its own unit (`PetFood::UNITS`), which is the honest shape for a bag sold in pounds and fed in cups.
