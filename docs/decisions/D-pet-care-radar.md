**D-pet-care-radar. The care radar takes a subject, not a user.**
A wellness exam is a checkup with a different noun, so `Health::CareRadar` needs generalizing rather than copying: its subject becomes a `User` (unchanged) or a `Pet`, whose events are the ones tagged with its lens. Per-species registries — dog (wellness, vaccinations, dental, parasite prevention, grooming and nails as `clinical: false` under D-nonclinical-care-kinds), cat (the first four) — and for a rabbit, bird, reptile or horse **one generic wellness kind and nothing more**, because we do not know an iguana's cadence and inventing one is the invented-cardiologist failure in a smaller costume; custom kinds cover the owner who does know. This is also the second consumer HEALTH.md §3 was waiting for on sub-month cadence: monthly preventatives and a six-week groom don't round to whole months, so the registry's `interval_months:` becomes an `interval:` duration and `due_on` becomes `last_visit_on + kind.interval` — one method, no branching, human behaviour unchanged. The correctness half is a *pair*: "Rex — dental cleaning" tagged Health currently classifies as the owner's dental and reports them on track, so a user's radar must exclude events tagged to any pet lens, and the pet's radar reads exactly those. Pets then join the Health page's household-care strip as rows that need no login ("Rex: vaccines due" beside "Maya: dental booked Mar 4"), and the vet is simply a `Person` with relationship "Vet", which makes "find a vet" `find_care` with the specialty prefilled rather than a new tool. Per-pet tuning (mute, interval, custom kinds) waits on making `care_settings.subject` polymorphic — mechanical, and deferred by the same discipline that shipped three human kinds before four. — *Built 2026-08 (W2), plus the watcher in W5. One thing the build changed: the plan inherited "own events only" from HEALTH.md without noticing it inverts for animals — a spouse's shared appointment is not your checkup, but the spouse who drove the dog to the vet booked the *dog's* visit, and there is only one dog. A pet's radar reads every visible event tagged with her lens, and because grants are per-grantor, a shared pet's page grew the reciprocal share button that makes one shared calendar possible.*
