**Q-intake-edit-in-review. Editing landings in the review page before saving.**
The review page (D-document-intake) is a checklist in v1: check, uncheck, save. A landing with a wrong date or a misread name is saved and then fixed in the row's own edit form, where every field already is — the D16 rule that a mis-filed item is low-stakes and fixable. Inline editing before the click is the obvious next rung and doubles the page's form surface for every kind (each kind would need a preview *and* a form partial, or the registry's field DSL would have to render inputs). Wait for real use to say which field is wrong often enough to earn it; the likeliest answers are a date and a title, and a two-field inline edit is a much smaller build than a general one. — *Open; parked with INTAKE_PLAN §5/§9.*
