**Q-education-jurisdictions. Homeschool-law mapping: per-jurisdiction requirements are a dataset, not code — when is it worth shipping?**
Homeschool law is a 50-state (and multi-country) matrix of notification, hours, subjects, and assessment requirements. The D-learning-record generators stay jurisdiction-neutral; a per-state requirements table is a dataset (the `datasets` service shape), worth shipping only when it can be maintained honestly. Until then the app produces evidence and the household maps it to their law.
