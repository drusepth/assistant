**D-mirror-layer. High-volume external streams are mirrored, not imported.**
Email (and future firehose sources: transactions, chat exports) lands in a per-source **mirror** — owner-private cache tables with real columns, outside the primitive pool, outside `Visible`/`Sectionable`, unreachable by grants, with user-configurable windowed retention (default 90 days, up to "everything"). Mirror rows become primitives only by **promotion**: an explicit user click or an accepted suggestion, with provenance recorded (`email_message_items`). Mirrors are not silos, though: they feed deterministic, read-only signals *outward* — messages match to People at ingest, outbound mail can (opt-in) advance contact recency, receipts/renewal notices can fuel watchers later — but nothing ever writes a primitive from a mirror without a human click. Because a mirror ingests **attacker-controlled text** that flows into the model, the hostile-input rules travel with the pattern: model output from mirror content is validated labels only, notification payloads are code-built from metadata (never model prose), and answers over mirror content cite their sources. Low-volume identity-shaped data (contacts, calendar) keeps importing directly into primitives as today. Mirror tables live in the dedicated mirror database (D-mirror-database). This keeps the lens model clean (D5), makes mail privacy structural rather than policied (D9 — no `visible_to` path exists to leak), and gives promoted attachments the whole existing pipeline (analysis, decode-letter routing) for free. Full design in `EMAIL_PLAN.md`. — *Built (E1a/E1b): the mirror, tombstoned deletion, promotion with provenance, People matching, and opt-in contact recency all landed as designed; the structural-privacy claim held — no mirror model includes Visible or Sectionable.*
