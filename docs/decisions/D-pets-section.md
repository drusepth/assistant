**D-pets-section. Pets get a top-level section, and every pet's lens moves under it.**
Amends D-pet-primitive, by request. Pets were reached by opening People and finding a strip of animal cards between the circle and the groups — janky to find, and odd to live beside "who you haven't spoken to". So Pets is the ninth top-level section (seeded, plus `20260923060034_add_pets_section.rb` for running boxes, which re-parents every existing pet's lens), and the pets strip leaves the People page for the Pets hub's lead panel (`Sections::Panels::Pets`): the pet cards, the Remembered strip, and routine care across every animal. The pet tools register on `pets` (and keep `health`), so they leave the People Act zone.

What D-pet-primitive refused still holds, because it was never about which parent the lens has. The refusal was a Pets *silo* — the vet visit filed under Pets instead of Health, off the care radar and out of the budget. That doesn't happen: the vet visit is still `Health + Rex`, Rex's lens is simply a child of Pets instead of People, and a section page browses its children, so the Pets hub's browse zone is "everything about any of your pets" for free. Slugs, taggings and grants on a lens are untouched by the move — only `parent_id` changes — so no item loses a tag and no share is widened or narrowed (section grants are per exact section, never inherited from a parent). A household with no pets now sees a Pets entry in the sidebar with one "Add a pet" tile; it turns off in Settings like any section, which replaces D-pet-primitive's "zero pets is zero pixels".
