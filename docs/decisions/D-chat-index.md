**D-chat-index. Chat runs get an index, because a conversation you can't get back to is write-once.**
`config/routes.rb` mounts `resources :tool_runs, only: %i[ show destroy ]` — there is no index anywhere in the app, and the only route back into a past conversation is a link somebody remembered to hand-place (the Health panel's "pick it back up" for the last check-in is the entire recall surface). So every other conversation is effectively write-once: you have it, you navigate away, and it is unreachable until `PruneChatTranscriptsJob` deletes it at ninety days. The continuity is meanwhile *already built* — `ToolRun#previous_recap` renders "Picking up from last time —" at the head of a transcript — so the app implements resuming a conversation and gives the user no way to ask for it. `GET /conversations` lists chat-kind runs (owner-only, newest first, last turn as preview, grouped or filterable by tool, since "my tutor sessions" and "my check-ins" are different drawers), `tool_runs/new` leads with "continue where you left off" when recent runs exist, and the sidebar gains an entry — which is also what makes the chat tools findable at all, rather than reachable only from whichever section panel happens to link them. Recorded as a decision rather than a bug because it is a premise of `CHAT_PLAN.md`: the surface is benchmarked against apps whose *first screen is the list of conversations*, and one route plus one query is the cheapest thing in that document. — *Built. `/chats` lists every chat run newest-turn-first (ordered by a correlated `MAX(id)` subquery rather than `created_at`, so a conversation you returned to today sorts above one you started today and abandoned), with two queries for the whole page instead of two per row; a task-bound run links to its task, not to a run page; and the filter row only appears once there is more than one kind of conversation to tell apart.*
