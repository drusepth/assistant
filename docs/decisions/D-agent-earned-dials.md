**D-agent-earned-dials. The Active dial is offered, never defaulted: the agent proposes its own tuning, up and down.**
Onboarding deliberately never offers Active — a first-run user has no basis to want daily notifications from an app that hasn't shown them anything — and its step-2 copy promises "flip it on whenever the app has earned it." Earning it becomes the app's job to notice: one sectionless, model-free meta-watcher (`dial_tuner`) makes two symmetric offers. Upgrade: a Quiet section with ≥3 accepted suggestions in 90 days → one `insight` offering daily-and-notify; accept sets the dial via a new `agent_level` action key in `Suggestion#materialize` (validated against `AGENT_LEVELS`, own `user_sections` row only); fingerprinted once ever per section, so a dismissal is a permanent answer — which is what makes the offer polite. Humility: a section whose last 6 suggestions were all dismissed with none accepted in 6 months → an offer to go quieter (down one level); fingerprint bucketed by year, so it can recur but never nags within one. Both are tagged with the section they're about, so they surface in that section's own Attend zone where the question is legible. The dial still only ever moves on an accept — D20 applies to the agent's own volume too. Design in `docs/AGENT_PLAN.md` §6. — *Built (A4). Two refinements from writing the counts: suggestions the world retired (D-suggestion-lifecycle) don't count as ignored — that is the app being right, not unwanted — and the tuner's own suggestions never feed its counts, since an ignored offer to pipe down must not become evidence for piping down further.*
