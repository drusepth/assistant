**D-food-suggestions. The ranking is arithmetic over foods; the model chooses among the top few and says why.**
D-pantry's rule restated over `food_id` and extended to the three suggestions a kitchen app lives on. *Ready to make* is the fraction of a recipe's non-optional ingredients whose food (or its `kind_of`) is in the kitchen, tiered ready / missing 1 / missing 2, tie-broken by the soonest-expiring item used and then by least recently cooked. *Use it up* is the same query filtered to recipes using something expiring soon. *Buy to unlock* — "what should I buy so what we have gets used" — is a greedy set-cover pass: for each food not in the kitchen, count the recipes it moves up a tier, weighted toward those using expiring items, pick, recompute, pick again; milliseconds in Ruby over a few hundred recipes, and every result has an "Add to list". *Plan my week* is a plan tool that arranges those candidates into `meals` as a draft that lands only on the click (D-agent-prepared-work). The model, when present, leads each surface with a pick and a reason and may propose labelled swaps and a draft recipe the box doesn't have (on the import review banner, out of the rankings until saved); it never changes the ranking. With no model every surface is still there as its ranked list. Exclusions come only from what the user wrote: per-kitchen diners with closed-set allergens (excluded by join) and a free-text diet note (handed to the model, or shown as a reminder without one — never parsed). — *Built 2026-09-23; F6 of `docs/FOOD_PLAN.md`.*
