**D-people-auto-filing. A contact's sections are the assistant's output, not the user's input.**
The People form opened on a picker listing every section and subsection — twenty-five checkboxes standing between "Mom" and a saved contact, growing every time anyone adds a lens (Hobbies alone contributed eleven). That is the tagging chore the lens model (D5) was supposed to spare the user, re-imposed on the one primitive whose whole point is that you add it in five seconds while someone is telling you their name. The tags stay — every cross-section view is a query over them — but they are **produced, not requested**: `CategorizePersonJob` already filed quick-added people (D16), so the form simply stops asking and the job runs for every contact, on create and on any update that leaves them unfiled. What replaces the wall is the shared **Advanced** drawer (`shared/_advanced`, which landed alongside this from the recipe side): closed by default, with the filing state on its closed line — the sections it chose, or "your assistant files this one", or "not filed yet" — so folding the picker away never hides whether anything needs attention. Three properties keep it honest. (1) **Manual still beats the parser** — the existing `AutoSortable` guard is unchanged, so anything you tick is left alone forever; the disclosure is the escape hatch, not a decoration. (2) **Unticking everything hands the contact back** — an empty section list is the one gesture that means "you file them", which is exactly what the auto-sort guard already tested for, so re-filing needed no new column or flag. (3) **No model, no pretending** — with nothing configured the copy says so and points at Settings rather than silently leaving contacts unfiled. The presentation generalized past People while this was in flight — every form that renders the picker now wraps it in the same drawer — which is the right outcome: what is specific to contacts is the *filing behaviour* above, not the disclosure that hides the checkboxes. — *Built.*
