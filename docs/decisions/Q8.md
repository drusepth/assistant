**Q8. Contact-recency signal for the stay-in-touch watcher.**
Resolved (M3.2): `people.last_contacted_on`, set by a one-tap **"Log contact today"** button on the person page — deliberately manual, because the app can't see your texts and shouldn't pretend to. The watcher only ever speaks about people the user has logged contact with (no signal, no nudge), fingerprinted per person per quarter; accepting opens the Reconnect tool prefilled. Inference from Events/Notes was considered and dropped — guessed recency produces wrong nudges, and wrong "call your mom" nudges cost trust. — *Resolved. Revisited in part by email (`EMAIL_PLAN.md` §7): outbound mail is observed contact, not inference, so a default-off per-link toggle may advance `last_contacted_on` from sent messages matched to a Person; the manual button remains the signal for everyone else.*
