**D-trip-costs. A booking's price is a fact about the reservation — integer cents on the stay or leg, rolled up by the booking bit.**
The first cut of D-trip-spine kept cost columns out on the lens-over-primitives argument: a trip's budget should be the Money lens over its dates. Half of that survives contact with the actual household; half doesn't. What a *booking* costs is not a Money primitive — it is a fact about the reservation, like its confirmation code, known the moment you book and meaningless anywhere but on that row — so stays and legs now carry `cost_cents` (integer cents, the house money rule; the `HasCost` concern exposes a forgiving decimal `cost` for forms that swallows "$1,240.50" and stores nothing rather than something wrong). The itinerary card totals them split by the same bit everything else tracks: booked is committed money, planned is money still waiting on somebody to click "book" — which for a household moving every two months is the budgeting question. The lens half stands: a trip's *wider* spending (meals, gas, the museum tickets) stays Money-tagged items over the trip's dates, and when budgeting grows into its full standalone tool, the outlook's committed-cost-by-month is the seam that should feed it — documented in `docs/TRIPS_PLAN.md` §2/§5 rather than built, because the consumer doesn't exist yet.
