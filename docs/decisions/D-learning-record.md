**D-learning-record. The learning record is schooling paperwork generated from data the hub already keeps — evidence, never accreditation.**
The homeschooling family's blocking artifact isn't the teaching, it's the *proof*: most jurisdictions want some mix of a portfolio, a transcript, and an hours log. Everything needed is already in the education spine — mastery transitions stamp topics, capstones are Documents — plus one small durable table, `study_logs` (learner, subject, date, minutes, activity), written automatically by tutor sessions and review sessions and by hand for offline work, because ToolRuns prune at 90 days and an hours log must not. Three generators assemble **portfolio** (curated artifacts + narrative), **transcript** (subjects, mastery, dates, capstones), and **hours log** as Documents the household owns, prints, and files; assembly is deterministic, and the model, when present, writes narrative paragraphs only. The app stays jurisdiction-neutral — it produces evidence, and mapping it to a state's homeschool statute is Q-education-jurisdictions. The framing is printed on the artifact and non-negotiable: a record of demonstrated work, not a diploma and not accreditation. Full design in `EDUCATION_PLAN.md`. — *Built (W5): study_logs written by tutor, quiz, and review sessions (review minutes measured, not guessed), progress_report landing all three artifacts as markdown Documents, working with no model configured.*
