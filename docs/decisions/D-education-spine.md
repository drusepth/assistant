**D-education-spine. Subject and Topic are new first-class primitives — a per-learner curriculum tree; the doing layer stays on the shared primitives.**
A curriculum doesn't fit the existing primitives: a Goal's tasks are a flat day-sized burn-down, while a subject is a *tree* (strand → topic → micro-topic) with state per node — mastered or not, when, and how proven — and a lifetime of re-visits rather than a march to done. So, on the Photo/Recipe precedent: `Subject` joins the primitives (`belongs_to :user`, `Visible`, `Sectionable`) with positioned, self-referential `Topic` rows beneath it carrying the mastery state machine (D-mastery-evidence). Subjects default-tag Education but multi-tag like everything else — music theory is Education + Hobbies, bookkeeping is Education + Career + Money — so the section stays a lens, not a silo (D4). **The tree is per-learner, never shared reference data**: the skateboarder's physics tree and the pilot's are different trees on purpose, personalized against placement and interests; two household members studying "the same" subject own two subjects, and comparing them is a consent-based household-view concern, not a modelling one. Per-learner also means private-by-default (D9) with the standard grant machinery — a kid's subjects shared to a parent is an explicit, visible grant, never ambient surveillance. The doing layer is untouched: studying a topic can spawn a Goal broken into Tasks, sessions are ToolRuns, recaps are Notes, the essay is a Document — the spine holds *what's known*, the primitives hold *what's happening*, and Today/board/Calendar/Dashboard pick the work up unpaid. Full design in `EDUCATION_PLAN.md`. — *Built (W1): the spine, the commons and both enrollment doors, the syllabus page, the hub panel.*
