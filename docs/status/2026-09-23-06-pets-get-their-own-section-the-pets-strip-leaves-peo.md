**Pets get their own section; the pets strip leaves People**: pets were reached by opening People and finding animal cards between the circle and the groups, which was hard to find and odd company. Pets is now the ninth top-level section (D-pets-section): in `db/seeds.rb` for fresh installs, and in `20260923060034_add_pets_section.rb` for running boxes, which also re-parents every existing pet's lens from People to Pets (only `parent_id` changes, so slugs, tags and grants stay put; if a user-made lens already holds the `pets` slug it takes `pets-2`). `Pets::Lens::PARENT` is now `pets`. The hub's lead panel, `Sections::Panels::Pets`, draws the pet cards and the Remembered strip (the same `sections/pets_strip` partial), then routine care across every pet with the Health page's chips; the browse zone below is everything tagged to any pet, since a section page browses its children. The People panel no longer loads or draws pets. The pet tools register on `pets` (and keep `health`), so "My pet is missing" and "Bringing a pet home" leave People's tools, and `PeopleLens` no longer needs to hide them. Deleting a pet and cancelling the pet form now return to the Pets hub. There's also an /about hub for Pets, and the pets feature page now hangs under it. D-pet-primitive's point still holds: the vet visit is Health + the pet's name, never Pets instead of Health.
