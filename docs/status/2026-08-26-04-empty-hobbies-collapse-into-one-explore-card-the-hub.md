**Empty hobbies collapse into one Explore card; the hub loses its paste fields**: the previous pass put every empty hobby's follow-form behind a disclosure, which fixed the wall of forms but kept the wall of cards — eleven cards on a real instance, nine of them saying "nothing planned yet", burying the two with something to show. So a card is now what a hobby with *something in it* gets, and the rest become one line each in a single **Explore other hobbies** card: still doorways, at the size a doorway needs. "Something in it" is feeds or engagement, deliberately never *unread* — a card that vanished the moment you finished reading it would rearrange the grid as you use it, which is the same reason the order stays taxonomy order rather than unread count. The catch-all earns a card on the same terms, so an Other Interests lens left empty after its feeds were filed stops drawing one. The hub also drops the follow-a-source field entirely: pasting a URL belongs on a hobby's own page, where somebody has already decided to spend time on it, and the lens page has carried that form since W1. On the seeded instance this took the hub from thirteen cards and nine paste fields to four cards, one Explore card with nine links, and none — the whole hub above the fold. `Panels::Hobbies` grew the split (`rows` → `cards` + `quiet_lenses`) and the card partial lost its no-feeds branch. One shared-file test moved with the behaviour: `sections_controller_test`'s "a quiet hobby card invites diving back in" now asserts the quiet hobby is a link in the Explore card and *not* a card, with the detail exercised in `hobbies_reader_panel_test` where hobby behaviour belongs (D37).
