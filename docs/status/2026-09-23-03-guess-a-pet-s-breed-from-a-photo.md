**Guess a pet's breed from a photo**: plenty of owners don't know their cat's breed, so the pet form's Breed box now has a small "Not sure? Guess it from a photo" link under it. It opens a panel: tap one of the pet's photos already on file (the profile photo, or up to seven library photos tagged with the pet's lens), or upload another. `Pets::BreedGuess` sends that one image, resized to 1024px WEBP with metadata stripped, plus the species picked on the form, to the household's vision-declared model: the same gate as `Photos::Caption` and document reading. Up to three suggestions come back as buttons ("Maine Coon", "Norwegian Forest Cat · maybe") with one sentence on what the model saw, and tapping one fills the box. Nothing is saved until the form is, and an uploaded photo is processed in memory and never stored. The file input has no `name`, so it can't ride along when the pet saves. It also works on a pet that isn't saved yet: the endpoint (`POST /pets/breed_guess`) answers the form rather than a record. The prompt pushes for the honest answer: most cats get "Domestic shorthair" rather than a pedigree read off a coat, a crossbred dog gets "Labrador mix", and a photo where the model can't tell returns no breeds and says why. Breed is free text the owner edits, not a row anything is filed under, so the answer is cleaned (short, deduplicated, at most three) rather than validated into a closed set; D-no-keyword-logic isn't in play. Without a vision model the panel isn't offered, and a hint line says what would turn it on.
