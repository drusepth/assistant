**A demo household with lore, so the app has something true-shaped on it**: `db/demo.rb` and `bin/rails demo:seed` build the Okafor–Lynch household of Millbrook, Ohio — Kay, who schedules surgery at the local hospital; Danny, laid off from an HVAC job in July; Ify, fifteen and homeschooled since the anxiety got bad; a staffy called Biscuit and a twelve-year-old cat with kidney trouble; and Kay's mother Ada, who fell in June. Three logins, roughly 350 records, every section and every subsection populated (ABOUT_PLAN §7, which needed a publishable household for screenshots since real data is private by definition). The rule that made it worth writing by hand rather than generating: **the lore crosses sections** — Danny's layoff is a Career note, a Money shortfall, a Health insurance question and a Rights claim for unpaid overtime, and it is the same event in all four; Ify's 2023 appendectomy is a health record, a medical debt, and a collections letter with a thirty-day window. That is the lens-over-primitives argument, and a demo of unrelated per-section filler would have contradicted it on every screenshot. Four things the build turned up. Sealed containers can only be written by something holding the password: the seed creates credentials and diary entries by unlocking a keyring and going through `Vault::Share`, because inserting rows is genuinely impossible — the friction *is* the guarantee, and a test now pins that Danny can decrypt the wifi password Kay shared with him. Times in a seed are wall-clock times in the household's kitchen, and parsed in the process default they land four hours out — a 9am church service rendered as 5am until the whole seed moved inside `Time.use_zone(household.zone)`. Trip owners are not trip members, and a credential's `name` is non-deterministically encrypted so `find_by(name:)` silently returns nil. And `demo:seed` loads `db/seeds.rb` first, because the taxonomy has to exist before anything can tag into it — which is also why `demo_test.rb` does, the section fixtures carrying only the subset earlier tests needed.
