**The Food section is built** (`docs/FOOD_PLAN.md`, all seven waves; §9 records where the build departs from the design). Food is the tenth section, seeded plus `AddFoodSection` for running boxes, and its hub leads with the next seven days of meals, then the live shopping list, what's in the kitchen (what's going off first), ideas, and a strip of recipes; the recipe box keeps its Vault entry. Everything lives in one `Kitchen` (D-kitchen) whose lists, pantry items, meals, foods and diners are rows reached only through `KitchenScoped`: a grant on a kitchen is the app's first write grant, the owner is asked once whether to share it, and every write broadcasts a Turbo refresh so a partner's phone updates in the shop. The in-store list moves a tapped row before the write leaves and queues it through a dead zone (Q-kitchen-offline), keeps the screen awake, and checking a line off puts it in the fridge (un-checking takes it back out). Matching runs on a per-kitchen food catalog (D-food-catalog): `ResolveFoodsJob` files new wordings from lists, the pantry and every cook's recipes onto foods in one batched, validated model call — a key once per kitchen, ever — with aisle, location, one-level "kind of", allergens and staples from closed sets; without a model each wording is its own food and the page says so. On top of it, `Kitchens::Ideas` is arithmetic (D-food-suggestions): ready to make, use it up, and buy to unlock as a greedy set cover, with diners' allergens excluding by join; a model only picks tonight's dish from the ranked handful (`IdeasPickJob`, cached against the pantry, not the list) and drafts a week that lands only the ticked nights. The plan is kitchen rows drawn as a calendar band, with a once-per-meal "make the list for this week". Four deterministic watchers (`pantry_expiry`, `meal_plan_gap`, `meal_needs` — which replaced a shop-detecting watcher that would have read event titles for meaning — and `recipe_rut`), `Landing::PantryItem` for receipt photos, a pet's food onto the list, each user's own food-shopping budget envelope on the hub, the data export, and an about page. Not yet: the grocery-email door, the defrost offer, the model's draft-recipe proposal, and the FoodKeeper shelf-life dataset.
