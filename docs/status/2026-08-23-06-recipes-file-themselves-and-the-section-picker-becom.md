**Recipes file themselves, and the section picker becomes a correction path (D16; `docs/RECIPES_PLAN.md` § Capture and edit)**: the recipe entry form ended in the whole taxonomy — 27 checkboxes, most of them asking a cook whether dinner was about voting, immigration or sandboarding — which pushed Save below the fold and handed the user a filing job the assistant does better. The picker is gone from the entry form entirely: `Recipe` joins `AutoSortable`, and `CategorizeRecipeJob` (`Recipes::Categorize`) reads title, blurb, source, up to 30 ingredient lines and the cook's notes, then files it into at most three sections — prompted to file on what the dish is *about* (a dietary angle is Health, bread is Hobbies · Baking, an attributed recipe is People) rather than dropping every recipe in one place. It hooks all three ways a recipe arrives: typed capture, paste/link import (enqueued after `ImportRecipeJob` fills the row, since at paste time there is only a placeholder title), and a landed share-link payload. The D16 discipline holds unchanged — auto-sort only ever fills a blank slate, so a hand-tagged recipe is never second-guessed; failure is silent to the cook and recorded for `/admin`; with no model configured the recipe just stays unfiled. The **shared `section_picker` was rebuilt for every form that uses it**, since the wall-of-lenses problem was never recipe-specific: it now collapses behind one line naming what the item is filed under ("Sections — Health and People", or "your assistant files this one" when nothing is set yet), and offers only the sections the household actually switched on plus their subsections — with anything already applied always included, because a picker that quietly omitted a live tag would delete it on save. Recipes render it on **edit** only, so capture asks for the recipe and nothing else.
