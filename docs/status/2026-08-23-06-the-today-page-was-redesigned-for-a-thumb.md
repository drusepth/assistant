**The Today page was redesigned for a thumb (`docs/DESIGN.md` § Lists)**: the page you open first was the hardest one to triage on a phone — five overdue tasks, each a wrapped title plus a meta line plus its buttons, separated by a hairline in `edge` on `surface` that has almost no contrast against it, so the card read as one column of text; and the two reschedule buttons were labelled with a "→" glyph, which is sized and weighted by the label around it and so renders as a hairline at `btn-sm`. Three fixes, all in the design system rather than in the view: **`.item-stack`** (+ `.item-row`, `.stack-label`) is the list shape for rows several lines tall — each row drops back to the canvas colour and sits as its own rounded block with air around it, which is the system's own "rows inside a card drop back to the canvas" rule carried to its end, while `divide-y` stays right for one-line rows; **`.btn-touch`** makes a row action a ~40-point target on a phone and a compact `btn-sm` pill above `sm`, so the claim pair splits the row's width under a thumb and shrinks to quiet furniture beside the title on a laptop, now labelled with a drawn `arrow_right` icon that carries its own size and stroke; and **`.btn-alert`** is the attention register (accent tint, not red — overdue is emphasis) behind a new **glance strip** that sizes the day in one line above everything ("5 overdue · 2 due today · 2 events"), each pill an anchor jump to the card it counts, absent entirely on a clear day. `Ui::Card` grew `count:` (a badge beside the title; zero doesn't draw) and `anchor:` (an id plus the `scroll-mt` the sticky mobile top bar needs). Two changes ride the row partials themselves, so they land on every page that renders a task: a late task wears a **spine** (`border-l-4 border-l-primary-500` — the left colour only, since `border-primary-500` would repaint the divider it shares with the row below, which is what turned the dashboard's dividers orange) and reads **"15 days late"** rather than "Due Aug 8" (`task_due_label`: the days either side of today speak in days, the rest of the week in weekdays, everything beyond it as a date, and a finished task keeps the plain date). Claiming was also extended to the **Unscheduled** list, where it matters most — an undated task has no date to move, so those two buttons are the only way it ever lands on a day. Verified by driving the real app at 320/375/414/1280 with no horizontal overflow anywhere.
