**Trips grew into the travel tool: planned-ahead trips with stays and legs (booked/planned), the hub's where-you'll-be outlook, the calendar stay band, and household trip sharing by membership (D-trip-spine, D-trip-companions)**: The request came from a household of digital nomads — a new state every two months — whose questions the photo-derived Trip couldn't answer: where will we be when, is the next place actually booked, do we have somewhere to sleep on the drive between, is the flight back for the wedding booked. So Trip's map box went nullable (all-or-none, still never form-settable) and a trip can now be planned ahead of going; it owns `Stay` rows (kind, check-in/out, half-open nights so back-to-back stays leave no phantom gap) and `TripLeg` rows (flight/drive/train/car-rental, a drive in your own car bookless by default so the hub never nags about it), each carrying the one bit everything tracks: `booking_status`. `Trip#timeline` merges them into the itinerary card; `lodging_gaps` names the uncovered nights; `Travel::Outlook` (CareRadar idiom) computes the current stay, next moves across trips, and warnings for anything unbooked or uncovered inside a 45-day lead — rendered as the hub's "Where you'll be" card and a sage per-day stay band on the calendar. Sharing is membership, not AccessGrant (D-trip-companions): companions edit the itinerary, comment (`TripComment`, the first commentable surface) and post photos (`TripPhoto` — picker filtered by the trip's *dates*, never the box; visibility via a guarded `Photo.extra_visibility_clauses` branch like albums), while the trip map and derived camera roll stay owner-only — Q14 held again. New: trips index grouped by phase, manual new/create beside the candidate flow (discriminated by `trip[source]=photos`), stays/legs/comments/photo-posts/companions controllers behind a `TripAccess` concern, `docs/TRIPS_PLAN.md`. 46 new tests; the full suite's one failure (`DiaryHabitTest`) predates the branch.
