**The starter shelf goes wide: ~150 feeds, 48 topics, and CI that fetches every one**: the empty state's stocked shelf (D-hobby-feeds §7.1) shipped with fourteen feeds across nine folders, which offers a new reader a choice between technology and world news — for anybody whose hobby is birding, chess, brewing, woodworking or knitting, nine topics is still an empty reader. `config/starter_feeds.opml` now carries 154 publisher-run feeds across 48 topics under the same rules it always had: no aggregator or FeedBurner host, no account or paywall, https only, and offered rather than subscribed. Breadth changed the shape of the feature twice. **The shelf is browsed instead of read** — each topic renders as a closed `<details>` (no JS, the fold the circle cards already use) in two or three columns, so what somebody meets is a page of topic names and the checkboxes only appear inside the topic they opened; ticking works whether the folds are open or not, because the inputs are in the DOM either way. And **hand-verification stopped scaling, so it became CI's job**: `script/check_starter_feeds.rb` grew an exit code, a host-serialized worker pool and a single retry per failure, and `.github/workflows/starter-feeds.yml` runs it weekly, on demand, and on any PR that edits the shelf or its checker. It is the only job in CI that talks to the open web, which is exactly why it is path-filtered off every unrelated PR — a publisher having a bad morning should not turn somebody else's branch red. The rules that need no network stayed in `Feeds::StartersTest` where they run on every PR for free: unique URLs, https, no redirector hosts, every row foldered, no source listed twice in a topic, floors under the topic and feed counts, and a ceiling at `Feeds::Opml::MAX_FEEDS` so the tail of the shelf can't silently stop being importable. Nothing about the import path changed — ticked rows still go through `Feeds::Opml.import`, so a 48-topic shelf adds no fetch posture and no parse path of its own.
