**The dashboard hero, redrawn for the phone**: the household hero is the first thing anyone sees, and at 393 points it was spending ~270 points to say very little — the four quick-add pills wrapped three-then-one (which reads as a mistake, not a layout), the greeting-plus-date ran to two lines, and the avatar cluster took a whole row of its own to show one face. Three changes. The date left the greeting and joined the facts footnote (`Sunday, August 23 · 2 members · Portland, OR`), so the eyebrow is one line and the name below it is the only thing competing for attention. The faces moved up beside the identity block, capped at five with a `+N` bubble so an eight-person household can't push the row sideways. And the four quick-adds became a `grid-cols-4` of stacked icon tiles — a real thumb target each, the *kind* mark (check, pencil, calendar, flag) in a tinted disc with a small plus badge instead of four identical plus signs, and the Goal tile still the filled one. New atom `.btn-tile` (+ `.btn-tile-icon`) carries the shape and folds it back into the plain `.btn` pill from `sm` up: one button styled responsively rather than a phone copy and a desktop copy, because the quick-add dropdown is anchored to a single element and two of them would fight over one panel name. `Ui::QuickAddComponent` takes `icon:` and `tile:` for it; the pill call site in `notes/index` is untouched. Two smaller things while in there: the card takes a soft `primary-100 → surface → sage-100` diagonal wash (the hero is the one surface in the app that gets to be scenery rather than a worklist), and a highlight's detail now wraps onto its own line instead of truncating — half a date is worse than a second line.
