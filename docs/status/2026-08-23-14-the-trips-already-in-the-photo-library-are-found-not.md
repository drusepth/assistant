**The trips already in the photo library are found, not asked for (D-travel-facet)**: the map answered "where have I been"; a travel hub's real job is "what happened on that trip". Photos cluster in time as well as space, so `Travel::Trips` reads the trips out with no model and nothing asked of the user, and the hub offers them to keep. Home is the pin with the most distinct *days*, not the most photos — one wedding can out-shoot a year of ordinary evenings, and the place you keep coming back to is the place you live. Photos over 100km from home are away; away days no more than three blank days apart are one run; a run of 3+ photos over 2+ days is a trip. Names come from the gazetteer: one place is that place, several in one country is the country ("Portugal" beats "Lisbon and Porto and Faro"), and somewhere it has never heard of gets its month. **A saved `Trip` is a name, a date range and a box on the map, and nothing else** — its photos are the ones taken inside the box while it ran (so photos imported later join it on their own) and its events are what was on the calendar those days, read through `visible_to` so a flight your spouse shared with you appears. That is what keeps Q17 deferred: a trip needs no join table, because dates and a box already say what belongs to it. Saving re-derives every stored number server-side, so a hand-edited form can move a trip's dates but can never invent a place nobody went; trips are owner-only, like the map and for the same reason (Q14).
