**Pounds and miles by default, kilograms and kilometres on request**: Weights and distances are now imperial throughout, storage included — `pets.weight_kg` → `weight_lb`, `places.radius_km` → `radius_mi` (default 25), `happenings.distance_km` → `distance_mi`, existing values converted by the migration. The vet-sheet landing asks for pounds and Ticketmaster is searched in miles. A Units card in Settings lets a user switch to kilograms and kilometres; `Units` converts for them alone, both ways (D-measurement-units).
