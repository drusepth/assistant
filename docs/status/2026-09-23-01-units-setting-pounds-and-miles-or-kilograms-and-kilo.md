**Units setting: pounds and miles, or kilograms and kilometres**: A Units card in Settings (imperial by default) now drives every weight and distance the app shows or asks for — the pet form and page ("Weight (lb)"), a place's radius and its subtitle, event distances in the radar and its nudges, the vet-sheet landing preview, and the pet context tools send the model. Storage stays metric; `Units` converts both ways with round trips pinned by `UnitsTest` (D-measurement-units).
