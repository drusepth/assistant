**Tasks page restructured around its two modes**: the page was straddling high-level life planning and daily doing, and leading with the wrong one — a grid of tall goal cards that put the Today column four phone-screens down a page people open ten times a day. The organizing rule that fixed it: **interaction frequency decides screen real estate.** The board is now the default surface, with active goals compressed to the strip D17 originally specified — one scrollable row of compact chips, ordered by needs-attention (a step in Today first, then goals flagged "no next step" or gone quiet, then the rest by recency) — and the big cards moved to a **Goals tab** on the same page, alongside a Paused shelf and the Achieved archive (D-tasks-two-surfaces). Not a mode toggle, and not an un-merge of D17: hierarchy beats modes, one section, two URLs.

Three product gotchas got structural answers rather than styling. **Percent lies for open-ended goals**, so the breakdown call now also classifies each goal's `shape`: finite goals keep the percent climb, ongoing ones ("make new friends") speak in momentum — steps this week, or how long since the last one (D-goal-shape). **The one capture box routes**: the same call renders a `kind` verdict, and a day-sized capture ("buy cat litter") files as a plain dated task instead of a goal with a progress bar — bare captures only; anyone who filled the goal form is never second-guessed (D-goal-capture-routing, amending D17). **The guilt museum is defused**: goals can be paused without ceremony, an active goal quiet for six weeks says so gently and sorts into the strip's attention band, and the agent's "still matter?" check-in is specced but deferred to the Milestone 3 dials (D-goal-pause).

The capture flow also changed direction: submitting the box lands on the new goal's page, where the plan streams in — planning flows into doing instead of materializing off-screen (and a converted capture explains itself there rather than teleporting). On phones the board's three columns became swipe panes under a Later | Today | Done tab bar that opens on Today, so present state is the first thing visible. The whole design — industry patterns, pain points, and what's deliberately deferred (yearly retrospective, habits, household assignment) — is in `docs/TASKS_PAGE_PLAN.md`.
