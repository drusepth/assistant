**Hobby chips are spotted in what you read, and naming one files its feeds (D-hobby-spotting)**: the "Add another hobby" tile offered a static fourteen-name list minus the ones you already had, first four shown — the same four on every instance forever, blind to the one thing the box actually knows about its user. Meanwhile Other Interests was filling with exactly that evidence, since D-hobby-feeds sends every feed the classifier can't place there. `Hobbies::Spotter` now makes the one model call over those unfiled feeds and stores candidate names as `SpottedHobby` rows, which lead the chip row with the starters filling whatever room is left; a spotted chip wears the feed mark, carries its reason as a hover title, and the row gets one line of caption because a phone has no hover and an unexplained "Birding" is a strange thing for the app to have opinions about. **What the user has read is the signal**: each feed is described to the model by the titles it has had *read* in the last 90 days and how many, most-read feeds first, and a feed nothing has been opened from is described as the weak evidence it is — a subscription says you were curious once, an opened story says it stuck. That is not the per-story classification §2 cut: nothing files a story, `FeedItem` is still not `Sectionable`, and the sample is the handful of stories the user chose to open rather than the firehose. The model still names hobbies and never routes stories, so a candidate's feeds are ones *mostly about* the subject — a general-interest feed carrying one birding story must not be moved under Birding whole — and two feeds still have to support a name, because one strongly-read feed is a feed, not yet a hobby. Naming the hobby is what claims them: clicking the chip, typing the same name, or accepting the nudge into Start-a-hobby runs `Spotter.claim!`, which moves that candidate's feeds under the new lens **in the request, with no model call**, since the attribution was made when it was spotted; `MapFeeds` still runs after, because it asks about every feed and a candidate only knows the unfiled ones. Both writes now share `OtherInterests.claim!` (add-only, plus the one mechanical drop of the catch-all), the inverse of the `absorb!` that already existed. `Watcher::HobbyFromFeeds` gave up the prompt and kept the loudness policy — one pending nudge, monthly fingerprint — so one weekly call feeds both surfaces and dismissing the nudge quiets that name's chip for the rest of the month. With no model configured or fewer than three unfiled feeds, nothing runs and the chips are the starter list. The old `hobby_suggestions` helper left `SectionsHelper` on the way out (D37).
