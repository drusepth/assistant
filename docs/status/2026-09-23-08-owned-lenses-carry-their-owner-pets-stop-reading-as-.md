The cause was that a pet's lens is a child of People and nothing on the row said so, so every surface reading People's children had to remember to ask the pets table. `sections.owner_type` (migrated and backfilled from `pets.section_id`) now says so, and every question reads it:
- The person page's group picker, the Address Book's group chips, the membership endpoint and the People-page cards share `Section.people_groups`.
- Settings and onboarding list `Section#subsections`, so "Book club · Dimitri" is just "Book club".
- A group named like a pet is a new group instead of the pet's lens.
- Chips, page titles and the model's taxonomy say "Pet · Dimitri" rather than "People · Dimitri".
- The section picker offers your own pets' lenses even with People switched off, and never another member's private pet.
