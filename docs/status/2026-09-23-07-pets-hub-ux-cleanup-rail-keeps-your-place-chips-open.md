**Pets hub UX cleanup: rail keeps your place, chips open the pet, tools compact**: a follow-up to the Pets section, from a pass over the pages as a user would see them. **The rail keeps your place:** `section_nav_link` now also lights the top-level section a page belongs to (`nav_section_slug`). That is a subsection or lens page's root, and anywhere else a page claims it with `content_for :nav_section`. So a pet's page, a pet's lens, Budget and a hobby's river all keep their parent lit, and the pet page has a "← Pets" link. **Chips open the pet:** a pet's lens chip reads "Rex", not "Pets · Rex", and opens Rex's page, not the thin list of her tagged items. `PetsHelper#pet_for_lens` does one query per render and only for the pets the viewer can see; anyone else still gets the plain lens link. The list of tagged items is now reached from "Everything filed under Rex →" at the foot of the pet page. The page's comment already claimed that link existed, but it never did. **Pet tools leave Health:** they stay registered there, but the Health Act zone hides all of them, since the Pets hub and each pet's page are their front doors. **Compact tools on the hub:** the Pets panel hides the six tool cards and draws one "What the app can do" row of buttons, like the pet page's row, with "My pet is missing" set apart. A petless household sees only "Bringing a pet home", and with exactly one pet the buttons prefill her. **Less repetition:** the "Your pets" label is gone, and the routine-care roll-up shows only from three pets up (`CARE_SUMMARY_FROM`), since each card already names what's due.
