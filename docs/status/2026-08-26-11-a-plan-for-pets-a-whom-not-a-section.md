**A plan for pets: a whom, not a section**: the biggest hole in the life-tracking surface — vet care, food, cost, papers, the photos everyone actually wants — with nothing in the app able to say *this item is about Rex*. `docs/PETS_PLAN.md` plans it as a blend rather than a silo, and the plan turns on refusing the obvious answer: a top-level Pets section would pull the vet appointment off the care radar and the kibble out of the budget, which is exactly the standalone failure. Instead a `Pet` record (species, breed, dates, microchip, vet, avatar) **plus a custom lens under People**, so "about Rex" is an ordinary tag — every form's section picker already files to her, `in_section` gathers every primitive including Photos (no `photo_pets` table), and sharing a household pet is an ordinary section grant. Pets therefore don't force Q17. `Health::CareRadar` grows a *subject* with per-species kinds, which is also the second consumer HEALTH.md §3 was waiting for on sub-month cadence (monthly preventatives, a six-week groom) — and the correctness pair nobody would have noticed later: "Rex — dental cleaning" tagged Health currently counts as the owner's dental. Food gets real columns, unlike D36's medications, because it computes: bag ÷ daily amount is a reorder Event, and the monthly total is a budget target. Five waves, six tools, and three code-authored guardrails on the pet-health chat (emergency notice, a shipped FDA toxics list rather than a model's recollection, a find-a-vet card) — plus `died_on`, because pets die and the app must not ask a bereaved owner to confirm a deletion. D-pet-primitive, D-pet-care-radar, D-pet-food-shelf; Q-pet-metrics, Q-pet-sitter.
