**Calendar: month, week and day views, drag to reschedule**: the calendar now zooms — Month (`/calendar/2026/9`), Week (`/calendar/week/2026-09-21`) and Day (`/calendar/day/2026-09-23`) — switched by a tab bar, with the one you used last remembered so the sidebar's bare `/calendar` reopens it. All three read one `Calendar::Agenda`, which puts each day in the order a person reads it: the untimed first (all-day events, then what's due), then events and reminders interleaved on one clock, timed events now wearing their start time. A month cell is one tap target that opens its day — the fix for the phone's 6px dots, which are now plain marks, and for "+N more", which was dead text and is now a link. The week shows everything each day holds (stacking into a list of days on a phone); the day view lists what's past due (on today), All day (task rows with their complete button) and the Schedule, and adds on its own date — the header's Task and Event quick-adds prefill it, and a folded form sets a reminder on it. Open tasks whose day has passed wear the accent spine and a warning mark, and a banner above every view says how many are past due and links to today. Past days sit back a little. Your own events and tasks drag to another day in month and week (`CalendarMovesController`, native HTML5 drag and drop — desktop only, since touch browsers don't fire those events): an event keeps its clock time and length, and relative reminders follow through Remindable's resync. The layer toggles became `.chip-toggle` links wearing `aria-pressed`, so the zoom (a single choice) and the layers (independent switches) no longer look alike.
