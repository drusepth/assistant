**Cards say where their padding comes from**: the school shelf looked like a page whose stylesheet had 404'd — a stack of sand rectangles with the text welded to the rounded edge — while every class on it was compiling fine. `.card` is the surface only (radius, tonal step, no padding), because the app's most common card is a list and a list wants its rows flush against the fold; the inset belongs to whatever fills the card, and `Ui::Card` supplies it (`p-5`) for everything that goes through the component. Three hand-written cards had simply never supplied it: the shelf's course cards, the Education hub's "Your subjects" cards, and every message in a mail thread. They now carry `p-5`, the house inset. The interesting half is that nothing rendered can see this failure, so it joins the phone-width pins as a source-level one (`test/integration/design_atoms_test.rb`): a `.card` must carry a padding utility, be a `divide-y`/`.item-stack` list whose rows pad themselves, or say the flush edge was deliberate with the new `.card-flush` no-op — which the calendar grid, the dashboard hero and the people directory now do. `docs/DESIGN.md` grew a **Cards** section stating the rule.
