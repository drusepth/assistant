**Standing reminders are built**: "remind me every Thursday night to take the trash out" is now one form on Today — type it, Repeat → Every week, tap Thu, 8:00 PM, Remind me — and the confirmation says which Thursday it landed on. All three waves of `docs/REMINDERS_PLAN.md` landed in one pass, each green on its own. **W1** pulled the ICS importer's RRULE parsing and expansion into `Recurrence::Rule`, a value object with no clock of its own; the importer delegates and its tests didn't move. One correction rode along: weekly rules now expand inside the *calendar* week (WKST, Monday by default) rather than the week relative to the start, which is what the RFC says and, more to the point, what makes the next occurrence a pure function of the current one for "every 2 weeks" — a standing reminder remembers nothing but the moment it fires next, and a test now walks every rule the picker can write from each occurrence to the next to hold that line. **W2** added `rule` and `last_delivered_at` to reminders; `deliver_at` keeps meaning one absolute indexed moment, now the next one, and the dispatcher advances it in the same conditional `update_all` that claims the row, keyed on the moment the job loaded, so a second job for the same tick or an edit that landed in between finds nothing to claim. Missed occurrences are skipped, never delivered late — a box off for three Thursdays sends nothing about them and the series survives — and a rule with an UNTIL retires the one-shot way after its last ping. The picked date is where the series *starts*, not necessarily an occurrence: "every week on Thursday" chosen on a Saturday means the coming Thursday, and one chosen at 9pm for 8pm tonight means next week's, never an error. `compose_deliver_at` learned to be a true no-op when no form supplied the parts, which the retire path had been getting away with only because the past-moment validation ran on create alone; it now runs on update too, for one-shots. **W3** is one form for every way a reminder is set or changed: date, time, a Repeat select, and weekday chips that unfold for the weekly repeats (`.chip-toggle`, a checkbox wearing a chip). Every row in a list opens in place — Change unfolds the same form on what was chosen — so "make it 8pm" or "actually, every day" is never remove-and-redo; `update` joined `create` and `destroy` for it, and the plan's "editing in place" deferral came forward under the streamlining ask. A small Stimulus controller pre-checks the picked date's weekday, follows the date while the chips still say what it set, and renames the monthly option to "Every month on the 19th"; with JavaScript off the chips are simply always visible and the server takes the date's weekday. Recurrence stays off Event and Task on purpose (D-standing-reminders); a task can carry a standing reminder today, and the chore-on-the-board question is Q-routines, open until a household asks.
