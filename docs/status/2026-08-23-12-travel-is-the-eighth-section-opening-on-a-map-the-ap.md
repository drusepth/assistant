**Travel is the eighth section, opening on a map the app draws itself (D-travel-facet, `docs/TRAVEL.md`)**: a section rather than a page because a trip is already five primitives (flight = Event, passport = Document, budget = Money, the friend you stayed with = Person, the plan = Goal) and sections are lenses over one pool — so Travel costs a seeded taxonomy row (`db/seeds.rb` plus a migration, since nobody re-runs seeds on an upgrade) and inherits tagging, sharing, Settings toggles and the browse zone for free. Its D37 lead panel is the geotagged-photo world map, which also gets its own page at `/photos/map` (linked from the Photos header). Three things decide its shape: **the basemap ships in the repo** — `script/build_world_map.rb` renders public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m land into a ~40KB plate-carrée SVG that inlines with `currentColor`, and pins are positioned by arithmetic, so drawing a map of your own life costs zero requests to anyone (a tile host's log for a photo map *is* the itinerary, and a map that needs the network is blank on a plane); **nothing is geocoded over the network and nothing is asked of a model**; and **the map is owner-only**, holding Q14 rather than quietly widening it — `Photos::Map` reads `user.photos`, never `visible_to`, so the member you shared a photo with still can't see where it was taken. Pins cluster greedily against a spatial hash at ~240km (London and Paris stay two pins), the viewBox crops the empty polar bands but widens for a pin outside them rather than dropping it off its own map, and a pin is an ordinary `<a>` to `/photos?near=lat,lon` — the existing timeline, filtered to that place, own photos only — so the whole surface works with JavaScript off.
