**Pet vaccination records**: a pet's vaccines are their own records now, not generic events (D-pet-vaccinations, `docs/PET_VACCINES_PLAN.md`). Each dose, titer test or vet exemption is a `PetVaccination` row with its own good-until date, product, lot, rabies tag number, injection site, series dose, vet, certificate and reaction. The log form is shaped like a visit: date, vet and certificate once, then a checklist of the vaccines the professional bodies list for her species (dog, cat, horse, rabbit; free text for everything else), each with duration buttons and no default. The pet page's Vaccinations card shows current / due soon / overdue / booked / no expiry per vaccine, reactions as a warning that never collapses, core vaccines with no record, and old vaccination visits with "add what was given". The keyword-matched Vaccinations tile left the care radar; a migration marked the old visits once with the `care_kind` the event already had. A new watcher (`pet_vaccine_due`) collapses one visit's worth of due vaccines into one nudge, nudges again on lapse, and retires on a new dose, a booked visit or a pet who's gone. Intake reads certificates (`Landing::PetVaccination`). The Health strip and pet cards show what's due, the pet tools read the record (reactions lead visit prep, the rabies tag goes on the lost-pet sheet), and the one-sheet record prints or goes out by an expiring share link — `Pet` joined the share-link allowlist, showing only the sheet and streaming certificates through the token.
