**Reminders folded into tasks** (D-reminders-on-tasks): there is one list now. Every task form, including the quick-add on Today and the new-task page, has *Due*, *Repeat* and *Remind me at* together. The quick-add folds them under "Due date, repeat, reminder". "Call the pharmacy at 4pm" is a task due today with a 4 PM reminder. "Take the trash out every Thursday night" is a repeating task that comes back to Today each Thursday and pings at 8. Task rows and board cards show the result as chips (↻ Mon, Wed, Fri · 9 PM / 🔔 4 PM), and the task page shows it under Details. This undoes the earlier "Today header: + Task and + Reminder" entry: Today no longer has a "+ Reminder" button or a Reminders card. A reminder must now be set on a task or an event. Events keep their Reminders card, minus Repeat. The plain-words box moved to the new-task page and fills the task form (`POST /tasks/parse`; `/reminders/parse` is gone). The schedule is stored on the task (`tasks.repeat_rule`, `tasks.remind_time`). Underneath is one reminder row marked `schedule` that `Task#sync_schedule_reminder` keeps in step, so no code has to guess which reminder the form owns. Repeat and reminder are independent: a repeat with no time is silent (`reminders.silent`). The chore comes back onto Today on its day and nothing pings. A repeating task is due on its occurrence. A new rule starts on the first day that fits it, today included. `come_round!(on:)` sets the due date to the day it came round for. Moving the due date moves only this time round. A data migration (`MoveTaskScheduleOntoTasks`) turned every pending standalone reminder into a task carrying it, and moved existing routines' rule and time onto their tasks. New icons: `bell`, `arrow_path`. The Calendar follows: its layers are Events and Tasks. The Reminders layer and the day view's "Set a reminder" form are gone. A task with a reminder time sits at that time with a bell. A repeating task shows as a dashed ↻ chip on each day ahead that it comes round (`Calendar::Agenda` `:repeat` entries).
