**Reminders folded into tasks** (D-reminders-on-tasks): there is one list now. Every task form, including the quick-add on Today and the new-task page, has *Due*, *Repeat* and *Remind me at* together. The quick-add folds them under "Due date, repeat, reminder". "Call the pharmacy at 4pm" is a task due today with a 4 PM reminder. "Take the trash out every Thursday night" is a repeating task that comes back to Today each Thursday and pings at 8. Task rows and board cards show the result as chips (↻ Mon, Wed, Fri · 9 PM / 🔔 4 PM), and the task page shows it under Details. This undoes the earlier "Today header: + Task and + Reminder" entry: Today no longer has a "+ Reminder" button or a Reminders card. A reminder must now be set on a task or an event. Events keep their Reminders card, minus Repeat. The plain-words box moved to the new-task page and fills the task form (`POST /tasks/parse`; `/reminders/parse` is gone). A repeating task is now due on its occurrence: setting a repeat moves the due date to the first one, and `come_round!(on:)` sets it to the day it came round for, so routines land under Due today. A data migration turned every pending standalone reminder into a task carrying it. New icons: `bell`, `arrow_path`.
