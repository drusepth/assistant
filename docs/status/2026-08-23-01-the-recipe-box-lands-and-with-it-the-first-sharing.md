**The recipe box lands, and with it the first sharing path that leaves the household (D-recipe-primitive, D-share-link; `docs/RECIPES_PLAN.md` W1–W3)**: `Recipe` joins as the eighth primitive — `Visible` + `Sectionable`, a top-level Recipes view in the vault group rather than an eighth section, so a recipe multi-tags into Health, Money, People and Hobbies like everything else. It splits its body on purpose: ingredients are structured rows (quantity, canonical unit, item, a normalized `item_key` for the pantry match to come, and always the raw line as typed) while the method stays prose, because the pantry match and the grocery list key off quantities and nothing joins to step 4. Capture is a paste — one textarea, one ingredient per line, read by a deterministic parser (`Recipes::Parse`/`Recipes::Quantity`: mixed numbers, vulgar fractions, ranges taking the low end, ~30 canonical units with aliases, trailing prep notes, `(optional)`, bullets), so saving a recipe never needs a model and the round-trip is lossless. Scaling is a link, not JavaScript (`?servings=8` re-renders the quantities and formats them back into kitchen fractions, leaving "salt to taste" alone); cooking is logged with one button and nothing else. `RecipeBook` is a container on the album precedent — curation, not a lens — and `Recipe` takes the matching fourth OR-branch, so a grant on a book carries its recipes with it under the same `grantor = owner` guard that keeps a future collaborative book from leaking a contributor's. The new mechanism is `share_links`: a read-only public page at `/s/:token` on its own bare layout (no navigation, no chips, no email addresses, no way in), for the sister in another state who will never have an account here. It is narrow by construction — the subject type is allowlisted in the model *and* in the controller (`Recipe`, `RecipeBook`), the 24-character token is the whole authorization, revoking is deleting the row, expiry is optional and evaluated per request, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` keeps forwarded links out of search, and a book's link opens its own recipes and no others. Public links sit deliberately outside `visible_to`, so they never widen what a signed-in household member can see. Recipes and books leave with the user in the export (ingredient rows and book membership inlined, dish photos in `recipe_files/`). The pantry (D-pantry) and the AI wave — paste-import, "what can I make tonight" ranked by deterministic SQL, a grocery list with no model call at all, and a week of dinners planned onto the Calendar through `events.recipe_id` — are designed in `RECIPES_PLAN.md` §5 and planned as W4/W5.
