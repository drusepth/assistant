**The Vault picker, and vault pages in onboarding**: a "Customize" link beside the sidebar's Vault heading opens `/your-vault`, one card per vault page (Address Book, Photos, Recipes, Documents, Notes, Mail, Passwords, Diary) with a blurb and a Turn on / Turn off button that saves immediately and updates the sidebar in place, the same shape as the Your life picker. The pages now come from one registry, `VaultPage::ALL`, and the choice is stored as `users.hidden_vault_pages` so pages added later default on. Hiding deletes nothing and leaves the URL working; Mail still waits for a linked email account. Onboarding step 1 now asks which vault pages to switch on, below the sections question (D-vault-picker).
