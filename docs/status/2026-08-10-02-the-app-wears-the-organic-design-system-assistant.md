**The app wears the Organic design system (`Assistant Dashboard.dc.html`)**: the imported design was not just a dashboard layout but a whole look, and it landed the way `docs/DESIGN.md` promises a retheme should — almost entirely in the `@theme` token block, so the ~500 existing uses of `primary-*`/`stone-*` across every view re-voiced at once with no per-view churn. The ground inverted: cards are now sand (`#ebddc5`) on a cream page (`#f5ead8`) rather than white-on-grey, which means a card separates by tonal step instead of a border (borders came off) and rows *inside* a card drop back to the canvas color; the brand ramp became terracotta, a `sage-*` second accent was added as a genuine second voice (the calm/on-track register), Tailwind's cool `stone-*` was overridden with a warm neutral ramp, shadows were re-tuned ink-tinted rather than black, and containers went over-round (`rounded-full` buttons/inputs/nav pills, `rounded-2xl` cards). Type is Caprasimo over Figtree, **self-hosted** (`app/assets/fonts/*.woff2`) so a self-hostable app makes no third-party request to render its own text and works offline — their `@font-face` rules live in `app/assets/stylesheets/application.css` rather than the Tailwind input, because Propshaft digests asset filenames and rewrites `url()` only in stylesheets it processes, so a `url()` in the passed-through Tailwind build resolves undigested and 404s (the layout now links both sheets, fonts first). Headings take the display face from an `@layer base` rule, so views keep writing plain `<h1>`–`<h4>`. The seven section identity colors were re-drawn from the two ramps instead of stock `rose`/`sky`/`amber` hues, which read as foreign objects against cream, alternating terracotta and sage so sidebar neighbours never collide; and overdue was reclassified as *emphasis* rather than error — it takes the deep accent step (`primary-800`), leaving a warmed red ramp for genuinely destructive actions.
