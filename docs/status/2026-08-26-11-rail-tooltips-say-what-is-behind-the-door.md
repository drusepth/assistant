**Rail tooltips say what is behind the door**: hovering Career used to produce a bubble reading "Career / Section / Life Lens section" — three ways of saying the word already under the cursor. That content had two audiences and served neither: expanded, the label is two inches to the left, so the tooltip was a no-op; collapsed (`nav-label` is `display:none` under 68px), the title is the only label there is and the badge and subtitle were noise beside it. The subtitle slot becomes `detail` and carries live state instead (D-nav-signals) — the one question a hover can answer is "is there anything in there for me right now?". New `Navigation::Signals` builds one short line per row in the vocabulary the hub cards settled on: a date beats a count, a count beats nothing, and nothing renders as nothing, so a quiet row collapses to its bare title rather than drawing a filler line. Sections get their next event ("Interview · Sep 3", "today, 3:00 PM" for the near ones) else what's in flight; Today gets the agenda, Tasks the board (open plus unscheduled, which is the pile only that page can fix), Calendar the next event anywhere, Dashboard a count of *lenses* needing attention rather than items, Conversations the last chat and when it was last touched, the vault rows their size plus the week's arrivals, Address Book its contacts plus birthdays about to land, Mail unread plus what wants an answer. The `badge` slot stops being a category name and becomes a state worth acting on — `Overdue`, `Needs action`. Passwords and Diary deliberately have no row: a hover must not leak how full a sealed container is (D-passwords-vault, D-diary-vault). Batched, because the rail renders on every page — one memoized `Signals` per render, `Sections::Summary` reused for all the lenses at once, the dashboard row computed from data those rows already loaded, and a vault pile grouped on a boolean so its total and its recent count come back in one pass instead of two `COUNT(*)` walks. Two things fell out of the audit: the icon-only rail was a column of *unnamed* links for anyone not using their eyes (the labels collapse out of the accessibility tree with `display:none` and the tooltip was never in it), so nav links now carry `aria-label` — a subsection by `qualified_name`, announced as "Money · Budget" — and the bubble is wired as the trigger's `aria-describedby` with its own title `aria-hidden`; and the `⌘1`–`⌘4` and `⌘[` hints were fiction, bound by nothing in `app/javascript/controllers/`, so they are gone rather than implemented (`⌘`+digit is tab switching in every browser, and a `g`-prefix scheme is a keyboard-nav feature, not a tooltip fix). 27 new tests across the service, the helper, and a rail render.
