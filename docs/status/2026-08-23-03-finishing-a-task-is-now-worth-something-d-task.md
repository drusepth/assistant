**Finishing a task is now worth something (D-task-celebration)**: the checkbox on the Tasks board — and on Today's rows, and "Save & complete" on a task's answer card — sets off a real celebration: confetti out of two corner cannons, a banner that pops and clears itself, and, on the bigger moments, fireworks and a half-second thump of the page. It escalates by counting, never by asking a model: an ordinary finish gets confetti and a one-line cheer, three-in-a-day (and five, and eight) gets the streak copy, the last open step of a Goal gets "Goal complete!" over the goal's name, and finishing everything today would have put in front of you gets the full works. The mechanic worth remembering is where it runs from: completion is a `button_to` PATCH and a redirect, so an animation started at click time is wiped half a second later — instead `Tasks::Celebration` decides the tier server-side and leaves it in the flash as data (`flash[:celebrate]`, a key `Ui::FlashComponent` now refuses to render as a banner), and the page that comes back plays it. The Stimulus controller fires on a per-celebration token rather than `connect`, because the app morphs on redirect-to-self and the rig is already in the DOM by the second task of the day. Both switches stay with the user: `prefers-reduced-motion` drops to a banner that just fades, and a Settings card (`users.celebrations`, on by default) turns the whole thing off in one click — a treat you can't decline is a nuisance.
