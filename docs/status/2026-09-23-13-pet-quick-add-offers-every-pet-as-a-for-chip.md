**Pet quick-add offers every pet as a For chip**: a task (or appointment) added from Rex's page was filed under Rex alone, and making it both pets' — cleaning the litter box — meant finding the full form's section picker behind Advanced. When the quick form is pre-tagged with a pet's lens and the viewer has more than one pet, `shared/_pretagged_sections` now draws a "For" row with every pet the viewer can see as a toggle chip, the page's pet already on. Tick both and it is one task that shows on both pets' pages, not two copies to keep in sync. One pet keeps the old "Will be filed under" line; gone pets appear only if already tagged. Helper: `PetsHelper#pet_tag_choices`.
