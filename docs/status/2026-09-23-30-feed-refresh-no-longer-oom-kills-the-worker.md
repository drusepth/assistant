**Feed refresh no longer OOM-kills the worker**: `RefreshFeedJob` was dying as `SolidQueue::Processes::ProcessExitError` (signal 9). `Web::Fetch` read the whole response body and only then compared it to `max_bytes`, so an oversized feed — or a small gzip that inflates huge, since Net::HTTP decompresses transparently — was buffered whole and the kernel killed the worker, taking its sibling threads' jobs with it. The cap now binds while the body streams (`read_capped`), and a declared `Content-Length` past it is refused before any body is read. Second half: a killed check never wrote `last_fetched_at`, so the same feed was due again at the next 15-minute sweep. `Feeds::Refresh` now stamps the attempt as a failure (`Feed#record_attempt!`, "the last check didn't finish") before fetching; success overwrites it, an ordinary failure only replaces the words, and a crash earns the usual backoff.
