**The vault's four open questions were answered on the 2026-08 product call** (`docs/DECISIONS.md`): **Q-vault-autolock** — keep unlock-rides-sign-in with the 12h window and manual Lock (a family desktop isn't a corporate laptop; an idle dial is deferred, not rejected); **Q-vault-collections** — sharing stays item-level, the household-folder key model waits for real demand on the Q15 pattern (bulk multi-select is the cheap fix if tedium bites); **Q-vault-breach** — offline only, no live HIBP even as an admin opt-in, because a box whose pitch is that it doesn't phone home shouldn't ship a feature whose selling point is phoning home; **Q-vault-audit** — resolved *and* built: `vault_events` (owner-visible, names and verbs only) logs shared/unshared plus the full emergency lifecycle (added/revoked/requested/denied/opened), surfaced as a Sharing history card on the entry and a Vault activity strip on the Passwords page with emergency lines highlighted, recording via the D23 log-don't-raise posture. Routine reveals are deliberately excluded — they're the owner's own clicks, `last_used_at` already answers them, and self-generated noise would bury the two events a household member actually needs to see; agent use (W6) is the next thing that belongs in the table.
