**The recipe box gets its front door, and share links learn a second trick (`docs/RECIPES_PLAN.md` W4)**: recipes now arrive the way people actually have them — a link or a pasted page. The link is fetched by the *app* through the existing SSRF-guarded `Web::Fetch`, never by the model (D-career-web); a deterministic splitter finds the Ingredients/Method seams and hands the model just the recipe, so the prompt spends its budget on the food rather than the nine hundred words of memoir and the comment mine below it. The division of labour is the design: the model's only job is to re-emit clean ingredient *lines*, and the app's own parser reads the numbers, so an imported recipe and a typed one are the same object with no second, softer parser to keep in step — and with no model configured at all the splitter *is* the import (the D13 AI-lite posture). Imports ride the document-analysis pattern (the row exists from the paste, the job fills it in, a failure is visible with a retry) and carry a review banner until the cook has read them. Component dividers — "For the sauce:" — are now `heading` rows: in the list where a cook expects them, out of everything that shops or scales, and out of the `item_key` column the pantry match will be built on. Share links gained an envelope: the same token asked for as JSON is an `assistant-recipe` document (a recipe, or a whole book) that another instance imports with no model call, so a sister's link pasted into your own import lands her book in your box — the only federation this app has, needing no protocol, no directory, and no account anywhere. **Photo albums joined the share-link allowlist** on exactly the same terms — the container, its own owner's photos, the stripped WEBP variants a household grantee already gets, never the originals or the coordinates — which is what the allowlist was for: a new type inherits the plumbing instead of growing a second mechanism. The cook's-eye pass: a recipe with no photo now gets a warm monogram tile from the two ramps instead of a grey icon, a dish photo can be picked from the Photos library (re-pointing the same blob, not copying bytes), the show page leads with a one-line meta strip and puts the sharing furniture after the food and out of print, the app grew a real `@media print` face (recipes are the page people print, splatter, and hand over), share links render an offline SVG QR for handing a phone across a kitchen, the library sorts the four ways a cook asks (recent, cooked, never cooked, quickest), and a book keeps the running order its owner dragged rather than reading alphabetically.
