**W3 is finished (`docs/CHAT_PLAN.md`) — the transcript now reads like a conversation**: **time dividers** mark any gap of an hour or more and every change of day ("Today · 2:45 PM", "Yesterday · 9:12 AM", "Tuesday · 8:30 PM", "12 August 2024 · 8:30 PM" — the day only as precisely as it needs to be), and the first turn on screen always carries one, because with a windowed transcript the top of the page is wherever the reader came in. The plan's tap-to-reveal timestamp was **replaced with something simpler and better**: on a coarse pointer the group's timestamp is *always* legible and on a fine one it stays hover-revealed — a tap gesture whose only affordance is "try tapping" is not discoverable, and one line per group of turns is not clutter. **Copy** rides the app's existing `clipboard` controller (no new one) and sits in that same footer line, on assistant turns only. The failed-turn retry attaches to its notice instead of floating below it as an orphan button; the jump pill gained an unread count ("2 new", cleared by pressing it or by reaching the bottom by hand); a send gives a `navigator.vibrate` tick where one exists; `text-size-adjust: 100%` stops the browser adding a second scaling on top of the reader's own (the app already sizes in `rem`, so the plan's "respect the font scale" item was **already true** — the bullet was wrong to imply otherwise, and genuine iOS Dynamic Type is a different and much larger problem this is not); and `.bubble` gained a reading-measure cap (`min(85%, 46ch)` from `sm` up — measured at 338px of a 704px card) so a reply on a laptop isn't a 120-character line. Two things worth recording. The unread pill turned up **the same cascade trap the rail hit once before**: putting an `inline-flex` utility beside `hidden` is two `display` utilities in one layer, where the winner is decided by Tailwind's property sort rather than by the order they are written — so the pill was permanently visible until the layout moved into a `.chat-jump` atom in `@layer components`, which cascades before utilities. It was caught by *looking at a screenshot*, not by a test. And the 16px pin written in W1 did its job for the first time — it failed when the second `(pointer: coarse)` block landed above it, because the regex had been matching "the" block rather than the one containing `.input`; the test now brace-matches every such block, and was verified to fail when the rule is broken and pass when it is restored, since a pin that cannot fail is worth nothing. Only the aria-during-a-stream half of W3 is deferred, on purpose: there is no stream yet, and that rule is about streaming.
