They went through the two ramps first (accent for now, sage for later), which was harmonious and too subtle: two tints of the same warm family read as one voice at a glance, and a traffic light doesn't. This is the one place red is not about destruction — it is "not now" — so it stays at the 100 fill with a 200 border, well short of `btn-danger`'s saturation, and `docs/DESIGN.md` carries the exception next to the "red is for genuinely destructive actions" rule it bends rather than leaving the doc contradicting the code.
