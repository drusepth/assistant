**User avatars are built (D-user-avatars)**: members upload a photo in Settings → Your photo (`PATCH/DELETE /avatar`, own-user-only by route shape, PNG/JPEG/WEBP/GIF under 10 MB, one 200×200 WEBP `:thumb` variant served everywhere while the original is kept for export); `Ui::AvatarComponent` is the single renderer — photo when there is one, the pre-existing initials circle when there isn't — and it replaced every hand-rolled circle in the app (sidebar, dashboard household strip, household member roster, Address Book rows, People hub coming-up + circle, item sharing controls, section-sharing rows, credential sharing, emergency contacts, admin per-member storage); a Person linked to a household account (D8) wears that member's photo while an unlinked contact keeps initials (no name-match inference, per Q8); the avatar rides the D14 export as original bytes named in the manifest; `test/integration/avatar_surfaces_test.rb` pins each surface so a new one that hand-rolls a circle fails the build.
