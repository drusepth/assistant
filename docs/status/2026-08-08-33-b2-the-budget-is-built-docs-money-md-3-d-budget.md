**B2 (the budget) is built (`docs/MONEY.md` §3, D-budget-envelopes)**: `budget_categories` are the envelopes *and* the ledger's categorization target (one list, two jobs — Needs/Wants/Savings-&-debt plus optional income rows, `target_set_by` guarding hand-set numbers, archive-instead-of-delete once a category has history, re-adding a name revives it); `Money::MonthSummary` computes the month fresh every time (the CareRadar idiom — per-category and per-group spent-vs-target, refunds netting inside their envelope rather than posing as income, uncategorized spending surfaced as unbudgeted instead of hidden, transfers excluded, pace measured against how far through the month it is); two D37 panels land the surfaces — **Money** (month-at-a-glance hero, group bars, quick-log, import, subsection tiles beneath real signal) and **Budget** (the envelope board: per-category meters with a where-the-month-is tick, inline target/group editing, add-an-envelope, empty states as front doors that invite rather than scold) — and **`build_my_budget`** drafts the whole thing from `Spending::Aggregates` (code aggregates monthly averages, recurring charges, top merchants; the model never sees a raw transaction row), validating every proposed envelope (numeric non-negative target under a sanity ceiling, known group, deduped names, capped at 15) before suggest-and-apply lands it — hand-set targets survive re-runs, which double as "tune my budget" by showing the model current envelopes plus this month's actuals, and the explained-budget Note carries a code-appended footer naming exactly what the numbers came from.
