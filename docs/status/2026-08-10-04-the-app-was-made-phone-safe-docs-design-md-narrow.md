**The app was made phone-safe (`docs/DESIGN.md` § Narrow screens)**: the new dashboard board stretched the page sideways on a phone — and so, latently, did 33 other grids — because a `grid` with no base `grid-cols-*` lays out in one *implicit* column, and an implicit column is `auto`: it sizes to its widest child instead of to the page, which is why `truncate` inside those cards never fired and every card rendered ~670px wide inside a 390px screen. Every grid now names its base column count (`grid-cols-1` → `repeat(1, minmax(0, 1fr))`), and the two atoms that could out-width a phone were fixed at the source: `.btn` wraps a long label instead of carrying `whitespace-nowrap` (and is never wider than its container), `.input` carries `max-w-full min-w-0` (a `<select>` sizes to its longest option). Two new atoms carry the shapes that were being hand-rolled wrong — `.row-actions` (a row's buttons ride beside the text on a wide screen, take their own full-width line on a phone) and `.capture-row` (a field and its submit, stacked on a phone) — which un-squeezed the dashboard's suggestion rows, Today's reschedule rows, the People circle, and every one-line capture form; the spending ledger's five columns now fold to two lines under `sm`, the hero wraps a long household name instead of truncating it, and a task's checkbox sits beside the first line of a title that wraps. Verified by driving the real app in a 320/375/414-point Chromium: **49 pages, zero horizontal overflow**, desktop pixel-identical. `test/integration/mobile_layout_test.rb` pins the two mechanical rules so the blowout can't come back.
