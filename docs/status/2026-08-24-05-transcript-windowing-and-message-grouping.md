**Transcript windowing and message grouping are built (`docs/CHAT_PLAN.md` W3's remaining folded items)**: the last two items of the chat bug-fix pass. **Windowing** — `ToolRunMessage::PAGE` (40) with `newest_first`/`before`/`window`, an "Earlier messages" seam, and a Turbo Stream that inserts each older batch *after* the seam then replaces the seam with the next cursor — ends a hundred-turn tutor session rendering a hundred bubbles of DOM on a five-year-old phone. Three things are load-bearing: the cursor is the **`(created_at, id)` pair**, not the id, so paging back agrees exactly with the order `ordered` renders in (an id cursor disagrees the moment two rows share a timestamp out of id order, which a test now pins); the seam stays in the DOM once history runs out — hidden and empty — because it is *also* the anchor each batch is inserted after, so successive loads stack correctly without knowing about each other; and the scroll anchoring is ours rather than the browser's, since Chrome and Firefox have it for exactly this case and Safari doesn't — `chat-scroll#capture` records `scrollHeight`/`scrollTop` on the click and spends it when the batch lands, which works because Turbo applies both stream actions in one synchronous pass so the MutationObserver fires once against the finished height. **Grouping** makes consecutive turns from one speaker read as one utterance instead of three events, which was the most reliable visual tell that this chat was homemade: the transcript's gap became the spacing *within* a group (`gap-1`) and anything standing alone adds its own `mt-3` — cheaper and less fragile than negative margins on the grouped ones — one timestamp sits under the last of a run rather than under every bubble, and the **tail** (the one squared corner pointing at the speaker) moved out of `.bubble-user`/`.bubble-assistant` into its own atom so it lands only on that last bubble. A group breaks on a change of speaker, on a five-minute silence (yesterday's last word and today's first are not one utterance), and on a notice from either side. Both verified in a real 375pt Chromium: exactly one page rendered with the newest turn on screen and the oldest behind the seam, the grouped run measuring 12px/0px/0px with the tail and time only on the last, and history loading at **0px of scroll drift** — a named turn holding its exact 66px offset across the load.
