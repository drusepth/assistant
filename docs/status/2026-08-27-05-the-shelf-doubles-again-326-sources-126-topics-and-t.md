The file is now grouped thematically rather than by accident of when a topic was added — related shelves sit next to each other, which is how somebody browsing finds the thing they didn't know to search for.

Two rules got written down rather than left implicit. **Every topic carries at least two sources**, enforced in `Feeds::StartersTest`: one source isn't a topic, it's a link, and ticking it makes a hobby lens whose river is a single publisher. And **religion and party politics stay off the shelf on purpose**, said out loud in the file's header. Every other topic here can be represented fairly by "the two or three long-running publications in that space"; those can't, and a default list that quietly ships one tradition's or one party's sources is making a choice that belongs to the user — who is one paste field or one OPML import away from whatever they actually read. The rest of the floors moved with the size (≥90 topics, ≥250 feeds, still under `Feeds::Opml::MAX_FEEDS`, which the shelf now uses 65% of).

The two surfaces absorbed the size without redesign, which is the payoff from building them for browsing in the first place: the empty-state shelf goes to four columns at `xl` and reads as a 126-line topic index, and Explore goes to three, with search doing the work it was always going to have to do. Both checked in a browser at 1600px. CI's fetch job got the headroom to match — 8 workers, 35-minute timeout — and it is still the thing that decides which of these 326 URLs survive: ~170 of them are new and unverified from here, so the first run on this branch is the real review.
