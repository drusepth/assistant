**Haircut tracking on the care radar (D-nonclinical-care-kinds)**: the Health page's routine-care panel now tracks haircuts alongside checkup / dental / vision, because it's the same question the radar already answers — when was the last one, is one booked, am I due — and answering it needed no new storage. One entry in `Health::CareRadar::KINDS` (haircut / 2mo, matching `haircut`, `hair …cut|trim|appointment`, `barber`, `hairdress`, `hair salon`, `stylist`), sorted last so grooming never sits above the care that matters more, and every existing surface picked it up unpaid: CalDAV sync and quick capture feed it, the "Counts as" select offers it, the Tune page mutes and re-times it, household care shows it, and `Watcher::CheckupCadence` nudges on it. Classification stayed conservative — "Nail salon" is deliberately not a haircut (bare `salon` never matches), and grooming can't shadow a checkup. What's new is the `clinical:` flag on `Kind` (default true, so the three medical kinds and every user-defined custom kind are byte-for-byte unchanged): it governs exactly the medical next-clicks. A haircut tile offers no "Find a provider" — find-care is a find-a-*doctor* tool and aiming it at a barber would be the invented-cardiologist lie in miniature — and no "Prep for this visit"; the cadence nudge accepts into a plain task instead of a tool; and two derived methods, `visit_noun` and `history_phrase`, switch the copy from "visit" to the kind's own name ("Last haircut Feb 2026", "No haircuts on record yet", "Log a past haircut"). Known limit, written down rather than papered over: intervals are whole months (`last >> n`), so a six-week rhythm rounds to two months and reads due about two weeks late — an `interval_days` column and a Tune page that speaks weeks is the fix, worth doing when a second kind wants it.
