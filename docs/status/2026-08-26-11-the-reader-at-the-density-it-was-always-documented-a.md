**The reader, at the density it was always documented at**: the river was designed for a laptop and let go on a phone — the desktop row was 34px and carried source, headline, opening words and age; the phone row was ~96px and carried *less*, which on a 390×844 device came to six headlines on a screen the docs say holds twenty. Three causes, all gone (**D-reader-density**). The row painted every section on its feed, and Other Interests is on every feed by construction (D-hobby-feeds), so a chip appeared on 100% of rows, carried zero bits, and was the loudest object in the line while wrapping to two — a chip now shows only when it discriminates. The line was `flex-wrap` below `sm` with `w-full` verbs, so the meta row took a line of its own and then wrapped again; it never wraps at any width now. And the hover-hiding was `sm:opacity-0`, which never applies on a phone: three text buttons on all 404 rows at ~12×30px against a 44×44 target, loud enough to be noise and small enough to miss. The unlock for all three is that **a row opens in place** — a plain click or `Enter` expands it with the blurb and Open/Save/Read/Task at 44px, so the collapsed row can drop its preview, its verbs and its chip at once. The headline stays a real `<a href>`, so a modified or middle click still stashes a background tab natively and *that* row still leaves the unread list; opening here marks read but comes back `is-open` (`from=expand`) so the re-render doesn't shut what was just opened. Identity moved from "World news | The G…" eating 45% of the meta line to a 20px monogram face hashed from the host — nothing fetched, because an icon service would be handed every site the household reads and a render-time favicon would tell each publisher when their reader opened the app, which is the leak `noreferrer` already exists to prevent. Clearing a pile stopped being all-or-nothing: rows scrolled fully past for a beat mark themselves read in batches, under a rail that fills, with one Undo that restores the whole batch (the endpoint narrows ids to the caller's own feeds, so a forged id updates nothing and the rest still goes through); swipe left reads and right saves by pressing the row's own button, and pull-past-the-top presses *Check now* — the gesture changes, the politeness rules don't. The filter that was a wrapping paragraph of every feed's title became a tree: a rail at `lg`, a bottom sheet below it, grouped by lens with counts and faces. Saved became a card shelf ordered by when you kept it, deliberately without images — a publisher's `<media:content>` is a per-article read receipt sent from inside a signed-in session, so that ships proxied or not at all. `?` opens the key sheet, and `r`/`A`/`n`/`p`/`1`/`2`/`Enter` join `j`/`k`/`o`/`m`/`s`. Measured on the seeded instance: **6 headlines became 12** at 390×844 (56px a row), and 21 fit at 1440px (37px a row). Three reader tests moved with the behaviour and nine were added, covering the chip rule, face stability, the day spine, the shelf, and the batch's authorization.
