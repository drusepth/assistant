**The reset stops listing its attachments, and the FTUE offers a pet**: two things the pets wave deferred, both smaller than they looked and one of them a pre-existing bug. `Admin::HouseholdReset` kept its "the storage panel reads zero when the redirect lands" promise with a hand-written list of model names — and a hand-written list of every attachment in the app goes stale: it had already missed recipe images and all three avatars, four kinds out of eight, added by three branches that had no reason to know the file existed. It now notes which blob ids are attached before the destroy and purges whatever stopped being attached after (**D-purge-by-diff**), so there are no model names to keep in step and the next `has_one_attached` is covered before it's written. Strictly safer than the list, too: another household's files are still attached afterwards, and a direct-upload blob mid-form was never in the "before" set — which is the trap the instance-wide unattached sweep has to stay gated behind. Measured before fixing: on a single-household box the promise was being kept *by accident* (the gated sweep caught the strays); on a multi-household one the bytes sat there until the cascade's jobs ran. The regression test attaches one of everything and was checked against the old code first. And the FTUE gained a fourth shortcut on its last step — "Add a pet", hidden once this user can see a household pet — rather than a fifth step or a question on step 1, where the subject is sections and a pet is not one. Also in: the `config/brakeman.ignore` for main's allergy-severity false positive, which was the only thing keeping CI red.
