**The event radar is built (`docs/EVENT_RADAR_PLAN.md`, W1–W7; D-place-subscriptions, D-listing-feeds, D-event-radar, D-radar-accounts)**: the seven waves landed as seven commits on one branch, each green on the full suite. W1 is the whole feature with no model: `Place` (gazetteer-named, radius + lead time, per user), `ListingSource` (an iCalendar feed or a page carrying schema.org `Event`/`JobPosting` JSON-LD, found from any pasted address by `Listings::Discover` with a Meetup-group and Google-Calendar rewrite table, polled by `RefreshListingsJob` on the reader's politeness rules with a six-hour floor) and `Happening` (a candidate, not an Event — distance computed when a row carries coordinates, out-of-range rows kept under a fold, withdrawal when a source stops listing something, a 30-day prune that never touches what the user kept); `/places` with the where-calendars-hide card whose Find-it links open a search in the user's own browser; `/happenings`, the river by day with Add to calendar, Task and Hide; the hub's Near-you card. W2: `Radar::Match` reads each new series once against `Radar::Targets` — hobby lenses with their briefs, an interest brief, a causes brief — in the `Feeds::RouteStories` shape, with "Not this" and its Undo, a For-you filter present only where a model can have matched anything, the "<hobby>, near you" list on each hobby page, and the honest no-model line everywhere. W3: `Watcher::EventRadar` under the Hobbies and People dials, five per place per scan, one per series, retiring on the date and when the world resolves the row, accepting through the same `Happening#add_to_calendar!` path as the river's verb via a `happening` key in `Suggestion#materialize`, and a new default-no-op `Watcher::Base#dismissed` hook so dismissing a nudge rejects the match behind it. W4: openings — the undated volunteer role, a task rather than an event, a Give-time card on the People hub. W5: `Radar::Windows` reads each located stay on a trip against the user's own places and uses the stay's nights as the window; the trip page's "While you're there" offers an unplaced stay as one click to add, and rows inside a window are Travel's too. W6: `meetup` and `eventbrite` in `LinkedAccount::PROVIDERS` — RSVPs and tickets are calendar sync over `Events::Import` (which gained an `events:` seam), a Meetup `STATUS:TENTATIVE` is the saved row, groups typed on the account are a shelf under each place. W7: `Listings::Providers::Ticketmaster` behind a per-place consent box, Education subjects as targets, Find-your-people's deterministic "follow the calendars" follow-up, the about page. Five things the build changed from the plan are recorded in §10; the about page ships without a screenshot until the demo household has a place to photograph.
