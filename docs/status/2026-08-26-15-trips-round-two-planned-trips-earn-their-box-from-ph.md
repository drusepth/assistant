**Trips round two: planned trips earn their box from photos, stays pin themselves on the basemap with a route line, double bookings and between-trip drive nights warn, and bookings carry costs rolled up by the booking bit (D-stay-pins, D-trip-costs)**: The pre-merge review's follow-up list, built. A planned trip whose dates have located photos now offers "Place this trip from my photos" — `Travel::Trips#covering` keys the detector to the trip's own dates and the box/cover/label store recomputed server-side, so the planned trip and the found trip become one record. The gazetteer runs in reverse (`Datasets::Places#named`, a lazy name index beside the latitude bands): saving a stay pins its typed location when it names a real town ("Boise, Idaho" finds Boise, bare "Portland" goes to the biggest population, the unnamed A-frame honestly pins nothing), and `Travel::ItineraryMap` draws located stays as numbered pins joined by a route line — riding `Photos::Map`'s `bounds:` seam over an *empty* photo scope, so the window logic and both basemaps came free and no photo is read, which is why every traveller on a shared trip sees it (typed-location coordinates are trip data; EXIF coordinates stay Q14's). The outlook grew two warning kinds: double bookings (two stays claiming one night, whatever trips they're on — the calendar band goes amber "2 places" on contested days instead of silently picking one) and the drive nights between chained trips (ends-to-starts within 3 nights, both trips being planned, any trip's stay clears it — the drive never has to be its own trip). Stays and legs carry `cost_cents` (integer cents per the house rule, `HasCost` for forgiving form input) and the itinerary card totals booked vs still-to-book — the budget-hub feed stays documented, not built, until budgeting grows its standalone tool. Also: the photo-post picker paginates and the hub's duplicate "Plan a trip" button is gone.
