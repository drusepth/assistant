**The attention strip, given the same phone pass**: the card under the hero had the failure the hero just lost — headline, tinted counts and the "Open Today →" link all on one `flex-wrap` line, which at 393 points broke into a headline row and an orphaned link row hugging the right edge with `ml-auto`. It is now `dashboard/_attention` (out of `show.html.erb`, which was carrying 28 lines of it inline) and reads as three parts. The status mark stopped being a blank accent dot taking 32 points for nothing: it carries the state, a warning glyph while something is behind and a sage check when the board is clear. Headline sits over its breakdown chips on a phone and beside them from `sm` up — the mark and its text are one block either way, which is what stops the counts from wandering onto their own row. And the link is a real `btn-alert btn-touch` button on its own full-width line: `btn-alert` is the atom for precisely this register (behind, not destructive), where a bordered `btn-secondary` was too faint to read as the card's one action and a solid `btn-primary` would out-shout the hero's Goal tile a few hundred points above. Two smaller things: the phrased digest is fenced off from the counts by a divider (that half is the assistant talking, the half above is arithmetic), and the all-clear copy split into "All clear" over "Nothing overdue, nothing due today" rather than one sentence that wrapped mid-clause. The "→" in the old link was also the glyph `docs/DESIGN.md` warns against — sized and weighted by the label around it — so it is `icon(:arrow_right)` now.
