**The Food section is designed** (`docs/FOOD_PLAN.md`; nothing built yet): a tenth top-level section, by request, aimed at everything Samsung Food does for a household. It is a hub over the recipe box — which keeps its Vault entry, the way Photos did when Travel arrived — plus three things recipes never covered: shared shopping lists, the fridge, and the week's meals (D-food-section). Those three live in one `Kitchen` container that a single grant shares, and that grant is the app's first one that *writes*: anyone you share the kitchen with can add to the list, check things off and update the fridge, live over Turbo Streams, and meals are kitchen rows drawn as a calendar band rather than an Event per dinner (D-kitchen, amending D-pantry's pantry-per-user). The recipe-to-fridge match moves off `item_key` string equality — D-no-keyword-logic's word list by another name — onto a per-kitchen `foods` catalog the model files each new line onto once, batched and validated, with aisles and allergens from closed sets and exact-text matching as the honest no-model floor (D-food-catalog). On top: ready-to-make, use-it-up and buy-to-unlock (a greedy set cover over what one more food would complete) as arithmetic the model only picks among and phrases (D-food-suggestions). Seven waves, F1 → F7; F1–F2 (the section and a live shared grocery list) need no model and are worth shipping alone. Supersedes `RECIPES_PLAN.md` §5 and W5–W6.
